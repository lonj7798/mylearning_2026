## Top Picks for LLM Architecture Curriculum

The following articles are most directly relevant to an LLM architecture course, in recommended reading order:

1. **Understanding Large Language Models** -- foundational overview
2. **Understanding Encoder and Decoder LLMs** -- architecture taxonomy
3. **Understanding and Coding Self-Attention** -- core transformer mechanism
4. **Why the Original Transformer Figure Is Wrong** -- corrects common misconceptions
5. **A Visual Guide to Attention Variants in Modern LLMs** -- MHA, GQA, MLA, sparse
6. **Coding the KV Cache in LLMs** -- inference optimization
7. **Coding LLMs from the Ground Up** -- hands-on implementation
8. **Building LLMs from the Ground Up** -- end-to-end construction
9. **The Big LLM Architecture Comparison** -- side-by-side model comparison
10. **Understanding and Implementing Qwen3 From Scratch** -- modern architecture deep-dive
11. **From GPT-2 to gpt-oss: Analyzing the Architectural Advances** -- architectural evolution
12. **From DeepSeek V3 to V3.2** -- MoE, sparse attention, modern innovations
13. **Beyond Standard LLMs** -- linear attention, diffusion, recursive transformers
14. **LoRA and DoRA from Scratch** -- parameter-efficient fine-tuning
15. **LLM Training: RLHF and Its Alternatives** -- post-training alignment
16. **Categories of Inference-Time Scaling** -- test-time compute
17. **Understanding Reasoning LLMs** -- reasoning architectures

## Why This Is Useful

Raschka's articles are among the best freely available resources for understanding LLM internals. They combine rigorous technical depth with clear visual explanations and working code. The catalog spans the full stack from self-attention implementation through architecture comparison, training paradigms, fine-tuning methods, and frontier topics like reasoning and inference-time scaling. Many articles include from-scratch Python/PyTorch implementations, making them ideal for a hands-on LLM architecture course.
