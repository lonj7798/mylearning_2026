# Insights — Distillation practice

- Distillation SFT from a strong reasoning teacher beats running RL directly on the same base model at the sizes tested. Fine-tuning open dense bases on 800K DeepSeek-R1 samples with no RL stage produces students that outperform large-scale RL applied directly to a base model ([[deepseek-r1-distill-synth]], [[deepseek-r1]]). **Result (single study)**, and it is the claim the 2025 open-reasoning wave is built on ([[bespoke-stratos]], [[sky-t1]], [[openr1]], [[open-thoughts]]).
- Teacher identity changes student scores at a fixed query set. With 1.89M queries held fixed and each teacher's responses regenerated to a verification score of at least 0.9, Qwen2.5-32B base students trained on AM-Thinking-v1, Qwen3-235B-A22B, and DeepSeek-R1 outputs score 84.3, 79.4, and 70.9 on AIME2024 and 65.9, 59.6, and 57.0 on LiveCodeBench ([[distillation-source-matters]]); in agentic SFT, GLM-4.6 traces give 17.2 dev score where three GPT5 variants give 7.6-8.2 ([[openthoughts-agent-glm46-teacher]]). **Replicated** across a reasoning and an agentic setting.
- The large open distillation releases scale unique prompts and responses per prompt separately, and report both counts: 1.4M long-CoT entries give AM-Distill-Qwen-32B a 73.1 average over AIME2024, MATH-500, and GPQA ([[am-deepseek-r1-distilled-1-4m]]); 2.6M unique prompts with one response each are released with their category shares ([[am-deepseek-r1-0528-distilled]]); SFT of Qwen2.5-72B base on about 5M DeepSeek-R1 responses, selected by a per-category verify-score threshold and the coefficient of variation of verify scores, reaches 79.2 pass@1 on AIME2024 ([[deepdistill]]).
- Distillation into a non-reasoning student can use the answer segment alone. SFT of Qwen2.5-32B on DeepSeek-R1's answer segment with thinking removed raises GSM8K from 83.7 to 92.2, HumanEval from 80.5 to 90.9, and GPQA-Diamond from 34.3 to 40.3 over training on the original responses ([[am-reasoning-answers-for-non-reasoning]]). **Result (single study).**
- On-policy distillation and pretraining-time distillation are distinct from trace SFT. Gemma 2 reports a 2B model averaging 60.3 on three benchmarks from scratch and 67.7 when distilled from a 7B teacher on 500B tokens ([[gemma-2]]); all three LFM2 checkpoints use cross-entropy against a 7B teacher's outputs as the primary pretraining signal over 10T tokens ([[lfm2-liquid]]); Qwen 3 reports on-policy distillation reaching higher scores than the off-policy-distilled checkpoint it starts from ([[qwen-3]]).
- Distilled reasoning students overproduce tokens, and the library holds two measured repairs. One epoch of SimPO on 10K self-generated preference pairs cut average output length by 37% on AIME24 and 57% on LiveCodeBench-Hard ([[sky-t1-flash-overthinking]]); LoRA fine-tuning on importance-pruned copies of the model's own correct CoTs cut average GSM8K CoT length from 313.11 to 180.68 tokens at γ = 0.6 ([[tokenskip]]).
