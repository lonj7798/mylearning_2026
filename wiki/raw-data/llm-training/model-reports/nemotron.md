## Verification
- Checked on 2026-09-14 against: https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11704 (arXiv v2, 2024-08-06).
- Duplicate resolution: both cards describe arXiv:2406.11704; [[nemotron-4-synthetic]] has 49 links from wiki/courses/llm-training and this card has 14, so the former is the full card.
- Corrections to the previous card version (all stated correctly in [[nemotron-4-synthetic]] and its recipe card):
  - "Reward head: regression MLP that outputs 5 floats"; "RMSNorm multi-attribute regression head"; "shared trunk + 5 linear heads" → the final softmax layer of Nemotron-4-340B-Base is replaced by one linear projection from last-layer hidden states to a 5-dimensional vector of HelpSteer attributes (§3.1).
  - "Preference optimization: DPO or reward-PPO (NeMo-Aligner supports both) … specific chosen algo per release disclosed in supplementary configs" → the report specifies Code SFT → General SFT → DPO → three iterations of Reward-aware Preference Optimization (RPO), and the checkpoint after the third RPO iteration is the released Instruct model (§3.3.1-§3.3.2).
  - "Alignment data: single-digit-to-low-double-digit millions of synthetic examples" → the printed stage sizes are about 800K (Code SFT), 200K (General SFT), 160K (DPO), and 300K (RPO) (§3.3.1-§3.3.2); the report does not state whether RPO data were regenerated per iteration.
  - "Synthetic prompts generated by Nemotron-4-340B-Instruct itself" → synthetic prompts are generated with Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1 (§3.2.1), which also generates the first alignment iteration's data (§3.2.4).
  - "two responses per prompt from the Instruct model" and "the multi-attribute RM scores response pairs" → responses come from multiple intermediate models or several samples of the best model; chosen and rejected are picked by ground truth or a verifier where available, otherwise by LLM-as-judge in early iterations and Reward-Model-as-judge later (Chat-Hard accuracy 0.87 vs 0.54) (§3.2.3).
  - "Pretraining compute: not separately disclosed" → the report gives 768 DGX H100 nodes, the parallelism layout, and the batch-size ramp (§2.3, Table 2); it does not give total GPU-hours.
  - Core Insight "a vector-output RM is a stronger judge than a scalar-only RM, and enables >98% synthetic data" → the report states that multi-attribute regression RMs separate helpfulness from artifacts such as length better than pairwise ranking models, with no ablation (§3.1), and separately that over 98% of SFT and preference data were synthetic (§3.2).
- Removed as unsupported by the source: "Loss: per-attribute L2 regression" (the report defers RM training details to Wang et al., 2024); "attribute weights documented in the NeMo-Aligner config" (the report gives no weights and does not name that config); Key Figures "HelpSteer2 attribute histograms" and "synthetic-vs-human ablation of data mix ratio" (no such figure or ablation in the report); "the pipeline is self-bootstrapping: each round uses the previous round's Instruct model" as a full description (the first round uses Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1); "the cleanest public disclosure of a full reward-model recipe"; "Nemotron's richer labels let it get more out of 10K examples" (the Llama 3 comparison is not in the report).
