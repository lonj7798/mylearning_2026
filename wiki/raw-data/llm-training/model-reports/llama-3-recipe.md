Starting point for a small general-purpose run: the only verified small-scale pre-training settings are the scaling-law runs (40M–16B parameters, 6 × 10^18 to 10^22 FLOPs): cosine LR with 2,000 warmup steps, peak LR 2 × 10^-4 to 4 × 10^-4 depending on size, decay to 0.1 of peak, weight decay 0.1 × LR. For the 8B model the report prints peak LR 3 × 10^-4 and uses a 128,000-token vocabulary with RoPE θ = 500,000. To judge a candidate dataset for such a run, the report's method is to anneal a 50%-trained 8B model linearly to LR 0 over 40B tokens with 30% weight on the dataset. The post-training modeling rows (SFT LR 10^-5 over 8.5K–9K steps, DPO LR 10^-5, β = 0.1, NLL coefficient 0.2) were printed for the 405B model or "our largest models" (§4.1, §4.1.3) and are not stated for smaller sizes; the 0.1% long-context SFT share is stated without a model size (§4.3.4).
