# SmolLM3: smol, multilingual, long-context reasoner
- **Core Insight:** Reasoning mid-training on 35B tokens for 4 epochs (about 140B tokens seen) degraded long-context performance on RULER, and a linear merge of the APO model soup with a mid-training checkpoint at weights 0.9 and 0.1 recovered the base model's RULER score up to 128k tokens (Off-policy model alignment; Model Merging).
- **Guideline:** When a reasoning mid-training stage precedes alignment and long-context ability regresses, merge the aligned checkpoint with an earlier checkpoint that retains long-context ability rather than retraining, because the SmolLM3 team traced the RULER drop to reasoning mid-training and to APO data capped at 24k tokens, and recovered the base RULER score with a 0.9/0.1 linear merge (Off-policy model alignment; Model Merging).
- **Authors:** Elie Bakouch, Loubna Ben Allal, Anton Lozhkov, Nouamane Tazi, Lewis Tunstall, Carlos Miguel Patiño, et al. (Hugging Face)

## Summary
SmolLM3 is a 3B decoder-only model trained on 11.2T tokens, released with base and dual-mode instruct checkpoints and a full recipe (introduction; Data mixture and training stages). The instruct model supports reasoning and non-reasoning modes selected by `/think` and `/no_think` flags in the system prompt, six languages (English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese), and context up to 128k using NoPE and YaRN (Model summary; Enabling and Disabling Extended Thinking Mode). The pipeline is: three-stage pretraining, long-context mid-training, reasoning mid-training, SFT, APO alignment, then model merging (section list). The post states the motivation for the recipe release: the community lacks fully open recipes with public datasets for dual reasoning/non-reasoning instruct models, most approaches using complex RL and proprietary data (Supervised Finetuning, introduction paragraph).

## Key Contributions
- A published three-stage pretraining mixture with explicit web/code/math proportions per stage and nanotron configs (Data mixture and training stages).
- Long-context extension in two 50B-token stages with RoPE theta increases, plus YaRN extrapolation at inference (Long Context extension).
- Reasoning mid-training as a stage targeting general reasoning without a target domain (Reasoning Mid-training).
- A dual-mode SFT mixture with synthetic reasoning traces generated by Qwen3-32B for domains where reasoning data was scarce (Supervised Finetuning).
- Alignment with Anchored Preference Optimization, a DPO variant, using Tulu3 preferences for non-reasoning mode and Qwen3-32B/Qwen3-0.6B pairs for reasoning mode (Off-policy model alignment).
- A merge recipe that repairs the long-context regression introduced upstream (Model Merging).

## Technical Details
- Architecture: Llama-style decoder with tied embeddings; grouped-query attention with 4 groups; NoPE applied by removing rotary embeddings from every 4th layer, following Yang et al. (2025); intra-document attention masking; weight decay removed from embedding layers following OLMo 2 (Architecture and training details). All changes were validated with ablations on the same 3B architecture trained on 100B FineWeb-Edu tokens (same section).
- Pretraining stages over 11.2T tokens (Data mixture and training stages): stage 1, 0T→8T, web 85% (12% multilingual), code 12%, math 3%; stage 2, 8T→10T, web 75% (12% multilingual), code 15%, math 10%; stage 3 decay, 10T→11.1T, web 63% (12% multilingual), code 24%, math 13%. Mixture ratios were chosen by ablations on 3B models trained on 50B to 100B tokens.
- Long-context mid-training: 100B additional tokens in two sequential 50B-token stages, 4k→32k with RoPE theta 1.5M and 32k→64k with RoPE theta 5M, both upsampling math, code, and reasoning data (Long Context extension). Upsampling code repositories, books, and long web pages beyond naturally long samples did not further improve RULER or HELMET (same section). YaRN extrapolates to 128k at inference, a 2x extension over the 64k training length (same section).
- Reasoning mid-training: 35B tokens from OpenThoughts3-1.2M and a subset of NVIDIA's Llama-Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v1.1 containing R1 reasoning traces; ChatML template with wrapped packing; 4 epochs, about 140B tokens; the resulting checkpoint feeds SFT (Reasoning Mid-training).
- SFT: 1.8B tokens, 1B in non-reasoning mode from 12 datasets and 0.8B in reasoning mode from 10 datasets; 4 epochs, about 8B tokens; BFD (best-fit decreasing) packing; loss masked on user turns and on tool-call results (Supervised Finetuning). Synthetic data was generated by prompting Qwen3-32B in reasoning mode with prompts from existing non-reasoning datasets, to fill gaps where reasoning traces were scarce: multi-turn conversations, multilinguality, and everyday conversations (same section).
- APO: Tulu3 preference dataset for non-reasoning mode; for reasoning mode, generations from Qwen3-32B as chosen and from Qwen3-0.6B as rejected (Off-policy model alignment). The post states APO is a DPO variant with a more stable objective and reports higher downstream performance in internal ablations, without numbers (same section).
- Regression and repair: downstream gains in math, science, instruction following, coding, chat, and multilingual tasks came with a drop on RULER, traced to reasoning mid-training and to APO training data capped at 24k tokens (Off-policy model alignment). The merge is a MergeKit soup of APO checkpoints linearly merged with a long-context mid-training checkpoint at 0.9/0.1 (Model Merging).
- Chat template: `/think` and `/no_think` flags; non-reasoning mode pre-fills empty think blocks as in Qwen3; separate XML Tools and Python Tools sections for tool descriptions; a metadata block with date, knowledge cut-off, and current reasoning mode, removable with `/system_override` (Building the Chat Template).
- Recommended sampling for the instruct model: temperature 0.6, top_p 0.95 (Extending thinking evaluation).

## Findings relevant to generality, long context, agentic training, and distillation
- Generality: the blog reports base-model win rate across 12 benchmarks and states SmolLM3 outperforms Llama-3.2-3B and Qwen2.5-3B and stays competitive with Qwen3-4B and Gemma3-4B, and that it places first or second on HellaSwag, ARC, and BoolQ (Base model). Per-benchmark numbers appear in figures, not in the text.
- Narrowing: the reasoning mid-training stage improved reasoning but reduced RULER long-context scores, the clearest reported case of a stage trading one capability for another (Off-policy model alignment).
- Long context: NoPE plus decay-mixture training at longer sequences with increased RoPE theta was sufficient for competitive performance to 64k; targeted long-document upsampling added nothing on RULER and HELMET (Long Context extension).
- Distillation: Qwen3-32B supplies reasoning traces for SFT gap domains and the chosen side of reasoning preference pairs, with Qwen3-0.6B supplying the rejected side; this is off-policy distillation into preference data, not logit distillation (Supervised Finetuning; Off-policy model alignment).
- Agentic training: tool calling is trained and exposed through `xml_tools` and `python_tools` template sections, and tool-call results are masked out of the SFT loss (Building the Chat Template; Supervised Finetuning). No agentic benchmark result is reported.
- Multilinguality: evaluated on Global MMLU, MLMM HellaSwag, Flores-200, and Belebele across five European languages (Base model).
