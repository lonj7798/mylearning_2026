# SmolLM 3
- **Core Insight:** For a small (3B) multilingual long-context reasoner, Anchored Preference Optimization (APO) beats vanilla DPO — and mid-training on 140B reasoning tokens matters more than post-training algorithm choice at this scale.
- **Guideline:** Before tuning your alignment algorithm, invest in a reasoning-heavy mid-training phase; at small scale it dominates.

- **Authors / Lab:** Hugging Face (SmolLM team)
- **Year:** 2025 (released July 24, 2025)
- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/blog/smollm3 — https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B
- **Relevant topics:** Anchored Preference Optimization (APO), mid-training, synthetic-data SFT via Qwen3-32B, smol training playbook

## Abstract
SmolLM3-3B is HuggingFace's 3B-parameter multilingual long-context reasoner with a fully open post-training recipe. The post-training consists of (1) mid-training on ~140B reasoning tokens, (2) SFT on 1.8B tokens (split thinking / non-thinking), (3) alignment via Anchored Preference Optimization (APO), a DPO variant. All configs, intermediate checkpoints, and the "Smol Training Playbook" are published.

## Key Contributions
- **Anchored Preference Optimization (APO)** — DPO variant that anchors the preference objective; chosen over vanilla DPO for the alignment stage.
- **Mid-training innovation:** 140B reasoning-token phase between pretraining and SFT — distinct from conventional two-stage pretrain → post-train pipelines.
- **Synthetic reasoning-mode SFT data** generated by prompting Qwen3-32B in thinking mode on existing non-reasoning prompt sets — cheap and scalable.
- **Full public recipe + checkpoints:** mid-training, SFT, and APO intermediate weights all on HF Hub (`HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B-checkpoints`).
- **Smol Training Playbook** — accompanying blog-book documenting the choices at small-model scale.

## Post-training pipeline
- **Mid-training:** 140B tokens, reasoning-heavy — between pretraining and SFT.
- **SFT data:** 1.8B tokens total.
  - 1B tokens in non-reasoning mode — 12 non-reasoning datasets.
  - 0.8B tokens in reasoning mode — 10 datasets with reasoning traces, generated by Qwen3-32B in thinking mode from non-reasoning prompts.
- **Preference / RL algorithm:** **Anchored Preference Optimization (APO)** — a DPO variant. No PPO, no GRPO, no RLVR in this release.
- **Reward model:** none (APO is RM-free like DPO).
- **KL / entropy handling:** standard DPO-family reference-anchoring (β coefficient in APO); exact β not surfaced in blog summary.
- **Rollout scale:** N/A (no on-policy RL).
- **Hyperparameters:** not itemized in blog summary — full configs available in the smollm repo.
- **Verifiable rewards:** none directly; reasoning correctness comes from Qwen3-32B-generated SFT traces.
- **Self-improvement / iterative:** no iterative loops; single-pass mid-train → SFT → APO.

## Innovations vs predecessors
Changes from **SmolLM / SmolLM2 → SmolLM3**:
- First SmolLM release with an explicit thinking mode (reasoning SFT).
- Mid-training phase added — absent in SmolLM/SmolLM2.
- Alignment shifted from DPO (SmolLM2) to APO.
- Qwen3-32B thinking-mode distillation as the SFT engine — new; SmolLM2 used a simpler instruction corpus.
- Multilingual + long-context scope expanded.
