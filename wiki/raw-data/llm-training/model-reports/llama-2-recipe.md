Starting point for a small general-purpose run: this report does not train small models from scratch below 7B, and its SFT and PPO rows are not scoped by size except the PPO β and RM learning rate rows. When a 7B or 13B chat model is fine-tuned in the same pipeline shape (pretrained base, vendor SFT data, pairwise preference RMs), the verified rows give SFT at LR 2 × 10^-5 cosine, weight decay 0.1, batch 64, sequence length 4096, 2 epochs with prompt-loss masking; reward models at max LR 1 × 10^-5 for 1 epoch with batch 512 pairs; and PPO at constant LR 10^-6, batch 512, mini-batch 64, clip 0.2, β = 0.01. The report used A100-80GB clusters and 1,418,091 Meta comparisons plus open-source preference sets.
