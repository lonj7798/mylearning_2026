# Phi-4 Technical Report and Phi-4-reasoning Technical Report
- **Core Insight:** A 14B model pretrained on a mixture that allocates 40% of tokens to synthetic data
  (Phi-4 Table 5) and then supervised-finetuned on 1.4M o3-mini-generated reasoning traces totalling 8.3B
  unique tokens reaches its reported reasoning ceiling after 90 GRPO steps over about 6K math problems
  (Phi-4-reasoning §4.2).
- **Guideline:** When the SFT teacher traces are strong and cover the target domain, budget RL as a short
  outcome-based stage, because in the Phi-4-reasoning run AIME improved by more than 10 points in 90 steps
  and further training produced no additional gain (Phi-4-reasoning §4.2).
- **Authors:** Phi-4: Marah Abdin, Jyoti Aneja, Harkirat Behl, Sébastien Bubeck, Ronen Eldan, Suriya
  Gunasekar, et al. (Microsoft Research). Phi-4-reasoning: Marah Abdin, Sahaj Agarwal, Ahmed Awadallah,
  Vidhisha Balachandran, Harkirat Behl, Lingjiao Chen, et al. (Microsoft).
- **Year:** Phi-4: 2024 (arXiv v1 2024-12). Phi-4-reasoning: 2025 (arXiv v1 2025-04).
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.08905 (Phi-4) — https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21318 (Phi-4-reasoning)
- **Source type:** official technical report (two reports)
- **Relevant topics:** synthetic pretraining data, pivotal-token DPO, o3-mini distillation, GRPO, length-aware reward, 5-gram repetition penalty, midtraining context extension

## Abstract
Phi-4 is a 14B decoder-only model pretrained for approximately 10T tokens on a mixture in which synthetic
data holds the largest token share, followed by a midtraining stage that raises context length from 4K to
16K, one round of SFT, and two rounds of DPO, the first of which uses preference pairs built by Pivotal
Token Search. Phi-4-reasoning is a supervised finetune of Phi-4 on long chain-of-thought traces generated by
o3-mini; Phi-4-reasoning-plus adds one outcome-based GRPO stage on mathematics only. Both reasoning models
report gains on tasks not targeted during training, including 3SAT, TSP and calendar planning.

## Key Contributions
- A pretraining mixture in which synthetic data receives 40% of training tokens from only ~290B unique
  synthetic tokens, giving 13.8 epochs over that source (Phi-4 Table 5).
- Pivotal Token Search: a binary-search procedure that locates single tokens at which the estimated
  probability of final-answer success changes sharply, and turns them into single-token DPO pairs
  (Phi-4 §4.3, Figure 4).
- A "teachable seed" filter for reasoning SFT: where ground truth is unavailable, plurality responses from a
  strong reference model act as proxy ground truth, seed difficulty is estimated from the agreement rate of
  weaker models such as Phi-4 or GPT-4o, and rubric-based LLM evaluators score the number and complexity of
  reasoning steps (Phi-4-reasoning §2.1).
- A length-aware GRPO reward with cosine scaling, separate length controls for correct and incorrect
  answers, format overrides, and a 5-gram repetition penalty (Phi-4-reasoning §4.1).
- A teacher comparison: o3-mini at medium effort had a similar effect to DeepSeek-R1 but was more
  token-efficient; o3-mini at high effort was stronger at the cost of longer responses (§3.2).

## Technical Details
- Phi-4 is a 14B decoder-only transformer with default context length 4096, tiktoken tokenizer, padded
  vocabulary 100,352, and full attention over the 4K context rather than phi-3-medium's 2K sliding window
  (Phi-4 §3). Synthetic pretraining data: 50 broad types, about 400B unweighted tokens (§2.2). In the
  midtraining ablations, inherently long sources outperformed artificially concatenated samples (§3.3).
- Pivotal Token Search filters target questions to those with 0.2 ≤ p(success) ≤ 0.8, then binary-searches
  the completion for single tokens at which p(success) changes sharply and emits the accepted and rejected
  token as a one-token DPO pair (Phi-4 §4.3, Figure 4). DPO round 2, called judge-guided DPO, uses about
  850K pairs with responses from GPT-4o, GPT-4t and Phi-4 itself (§4.2).
- Phi-4-reasoning architecture changes (§3): two placeholder base-model tokens repurposed as `<think>` and
  `</think>`, RoPE base frequency doubled, maximum training length 32K.
- GRPO reward (Phi-4-reasoning §4.1): length-aware accuracy with L_max = 31,744, L_pos_control = 25,600,
  L_neg_control = 3,702; correct answers score in [0.5, 1.0] and incorrect answers in [-1.0, -0.5], both by
  cosine scaling; a missing `<|im_end|>` token overrides the score to -0.5 and a missing or malformed
  `<think>` block to -1.0; a 5-gram repetition penalty R_rep is combined as
  R_final = (8/13)·R_acc_scaled + (1/13)·R_rep.
- RL seed data (§4): 72,401 mathematical problems without solutions, 64 seeds subsampled per RL iteration,
  no coding exercises. Responses beyond 31K tokens are clipped during GRPO, which the report names as a
  limit on how much GRPO can help (§4.2).

## Findings relevant to generality and distillation
- Generality from reasoning SFT (Phi-4-reasoning §1): both reasoning models improve over Phi-4 by more than
  50 percentage points on AIME 2025 and OmniMath, more than 25 points on LiveCodeBench, and 30 to 60 points
  on TSP, 3SAT and BA-Calendar planning, which the report calls nominally out-of-domain.
- General-purpose transfer (§1, Table 2): Phi-4-reasoning-plus is 22 points more accurate than Phi-4 on
  IFEval, 16 points better on FlenQA long-context question answering, and 10 points better on ArenaHard.
  No catastrophic forgetting was observed after an SFT stage with more than two passes over the reasoning
  data sources (§3).
- Distillation teacher choice (§3.2): o3-mini medium effort had a similar effect to DeepSeek-R1 but was more
  token-efficient; o3-mini high effort was consistently stronger and produced longer responses.
- Measurement caution (§1): two runs of average-of-5 evaluations can differ by up to 5-10 points on AIME, so
  the report uses 50-64 generations for AIME 2025 and reports standard deviations. Phi-4-reasoning-plus uses
  roughly 1.5× more tokens on math than Phi-4-reasoning, with a smaller difference on other domains.
