# Insights — Agentic data and RL

- Small numbers of verified trajectories move agentic scores by large amounts. 491 successful trajectories sampled inside SWE-Gym raise the 32B SWE-Bench Verified resolve rate from 7.0% to 20.6% ([[swe-gym]]); 3,321 successful Sonnet-3.5-v2 trajectories reach 34.4% Pass@1 ([[r2e-gym]]); 500 GPT-4 ReAct trajectories raise Llama-2-7B HotpotQA EM from 14.8 to 26.2 ([[fireact]]); 1,866 filtered GPT-4 trajectories mixed 0.2/0.8 with ShareGPT raise a 70B held-out agent score by 176% ([[agenttuning]], [[agent-flan]], [[agentinstruct]]). **Replicated.**
- Trajectory supply is the constraint, and the library holds four ways to manufacture it: replaying mined web tutorials with an agent and keeping evaluator-approved runs, at 10,398 trajectories for $0.551 each ([[agenttrek]]); proposing and verifying tasks on live websites, keeping 94K of 175K runs at $0.28 each ([[explorer]]); relabeling sub-trajectories with the instruction they actually accomplished, turning 1,125-4,568 raw trajectories into 10,169-11,782 examples ([[learn-by-interact]]); and interaction-first synthesis, where 1K trajectories give 17.41% on AndroidWorld against 6.25% for the same amount of task-driven data ([[os-genesis]], [[agent-early-experience]]).
- Stitched or synthetic tool-call traces underperform real end-to-end executions. With Qwen3-4B-Instruct and a code interpreter, SFT on 3K real end-to-end tool-use trajectories gives AIME2025 average@32 of 29.79% against 3.65% for stitched synthetic trajectories ([[demystifying-agentic-rl]]). Teacher choice dominates within the same task source: GLM-4.6 traces give a dev score of 17.2 where GPT5, GPT5-Mini, and GPT5-Nano give 8.0, 7.6, and 8.2 ([[openthoughts-agent-glm46-teacher]]).
- RL on executable environments works without a value network and generalizes across environments. Starting from Qwen3-32B at 24.4% Pass@1, RL alone on 4.5K R2E-Gym instances reaches 39.4% in 4,601 H100 hours ([[skyrl-agent]]) and 42.2% in the DeepSWE run ([[deepswe]]); one Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct policy trained on five environments at once averages 67.7 task success against 67.8 for the best of five single-task policies ([[agentrl]], [[agent-world]], [[agentgym-rl]]); LOOP reaches 71.3 goal completion on AppWorld with LoRA and no value network ([[loop-appworld]], [[gigpo-verl-agent]], [[eto-trial-and-error]]).
- Agentic training has measured side effects on safety and on tool honesty. Fine-tuning Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on benign web-navigation demonstrations raised WebArena-Lite success from 2.42% to 22.42% and WebDojo harmful-task attack success from 32.88% to 64.38% ([[agentic-finetuning-misalignment]]); think-then-act GRPO on SynTool raised the No-Tool-Available hallucination rate from 34.8% to 90.2% ([[reasoning-trap-tool-hallucination]]); RL against one frozen user simulator peaks at 46.1% held-out success and then regresses ([[simulator-collapse]]). **Replicated** as a class of narrowing effects.
- Agentic evaluation is unstable in ways that change conclusions. Across 21,730 rollouts, higher reasoning effort gave equal or lower accuracy in 21 of 36 model-agent-benchmark combinations ([[holistic-agent-leaderboard]]); under one scaffold GPT-5 has the best Gaia2 overall pass@1 at 42.1 but 0.0 on the Time split, which rises to 34.4 when generation latency is removed ([[are-gaia2]], [[swe-bench-pro]], [[bfcl]]).
