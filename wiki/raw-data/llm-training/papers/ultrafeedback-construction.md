# UltraFeedback Construction: Boosting LMs with Scaled AI Feedback (pipeline-focused)
- **Core Insight:** Scale AI feedback by (1) sourcing prompts from diverse public datasets, (2) generating 4 responses per prompt from a heterogeneous fleet of 17 models spanning capability tiers, (3) asking GPT-4 to rate each response on 4 orthogonal aspects (instruction-following, truthfulness, honesty, helpfulness) with numerical + natural-language feedback, then (4) binarizing into pairwise preferences.
- **Guideline:** To build an "UltraFeedback-style" preference dataset from scratch, collect ~60K diverse prompts, generate 4 responses per prompt from a *heterogeneous* model pool (strong and weak), have GPT-4 produce 4-aspect ratings with rationales, and derive chosen/rejected pairs either by per-aspect top-vs-bottom or by overall score.
- **Authors:** Ganqu Cui, Lifan Yuan, Ning Ding, Guanming Yao, Bingxiang He, Wei Zhu, Yuan Ni, Guotong Xie, Ruobing Xie, Yankai Lin, Zhiyuan Liu, Maosong Sun (Tsinghua + OpenBMB)
- **Year:** 2023/2024
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01377
- **Relevant topics:** synthetic preference construction, GPT-4 as judge, aspect-based evaluation, preference datasets

## Abstract
UltraFeedback is the largest single open preference dataset of its era: 64K prompts × 4 responses = 256K rated samples, with >1M individual GPT-4 feedback entries across 4 aspects. The construction is important in itself: prompts pulled from UltraChat + ShareGPT + Evol-Instruct + TruthfulQA + FalseQA + FLAN; responses generated by 17 different models (GPT-4, GPT-3.5, Llama-2 variants, Vicuna, MPT, Falcon, Alpaca, WizardLM, etc.) chosen to span capability tiers; each response annotated by GPT-4 on instruction-following, truthfulness, honesty, helpfulness with both numerical ratings (0–10) and natural-language rationales. The paper-released binarized subset has become the default DPO / RM training corpus for open alignment.

## Key Contributions
- **Prompt diversity by design** — 6 heterogeneous public sources, explicit deduplication.
- **Model-fleet diversity** — 17 response generators deliberately spanning capability; guarantees preference gaps.
- **Multi-aspect GPT-4 rubric** (4 aspects) with numeric + prose feedback.
- Scale: 64K prompts, 256K responses, 1M+ aspect feedback entries.
- Became the downstream training substrate for Zephyr, Tulu, Starling, and countless open models.

## Synthesis pipeline (REQUIRED — be concrete)
- **Seed input — prompts (~64K):**
  - UltraChat (diverse multi-turn),
  - ShareGPT (real user logs),
  - Evol-Instruct (complexity-evolved),
  - TruthfulQA (truthfulness stress),
  - FalseQA (intentionally false premises),
  - FLAN (instruction diversity).
  - Deduplicated + filtered for length/quality.

- **Response generation:** for each prompt, query 4 out of 17 models with randomized system prompts covering helpful/honest/harmless/expert styles. Models span:
  - Top-tier: GPT-4, GPT-3.5-turbo,
  - Open strong: Llama-2-70B-chat, WizardLM-70B, Vicuna-33B,
  - Mid-tier: Llama-2-13B/7B-chat, MPT-30B-chat, Falcon-40B-instruct,
  - Weaker: Alpaca-7B, Pythia-12B, StableLM, Dolly, Starchat.
  - Mix ensures wide quality spread → clean preference gaps.

- **Annotation (GPT-4):**
  - For each (prompt, response) pair, GPT-4 rates 4 aspects:
    - **Instruction-following** (0–10),
    - **Truthfulness** (0–10),
    - **Honesty** (did it admit uncertainty?) (0–10),
    - **Helpfulness** (0–10).
  - Each score accompanied by a short natural-language rationale.
  - Overall score optionally derived from aspect averages.

- **Binarization:**
  - Per-aspect pairwise prefs (chosen = higher-aspect-score) *or* overall pref (chosen = higher overall).
  - HuggingFace's `ultrafeedback_binarized` variant uses overall score with `chosen = argmax, rejected = random-not-chosen` (intentionally not worst-of-4, to avoid degenerate negatives).

- **Filtering/rescoring:** drop prompts with all-identical scores; drop clearly malformed responses.

- **Output shape:** 64K × 4 responses × 4 aspect scores + rationales. Binarized subset ≈ 62K pairs.

- **Teacher model(s):** GPT-4 as sole judge; 17 models as generators.

- **Cost estimate:** >1M GPT-4 annotations ≈ tens of thousands USD at 2023 pricing.

## Quality / diversity evaluation
- Used to train Zephyr-7B-β (HuggingFace H4) — at release was SOTA open 7B on MT-Bench (7.34).
- Used in Tulu 2, Starling-7B, and dozens of community fine-tunes.
- Ablations show aspect-level prefs outperform overall-score prefs when target capability is specific (truthfulness, helpfulness).

## Risks + gotchas
- **Judge-induced bias:** GPT-4 scoring patterns (length bias, helpfulness bias, style bias) propagate into downstream DPO models.
- **Aspect conflation:** the 4 aspects are correlated; aggregating into a single pref loses signal.
- **Model-fleet contamination:** GPT-4 responses in the generator pool mean the judge is rating its own outputs — subtle advantage to GPT-4 responses.
- **Prompt leakage:** TruthfulQA prompts are public benchmark; training on them risks contamination (the binarized subset sometimes excludes TruthfulQA to mitigate).
- **2025 replacements:** newer Direct-Judgement-Preference-Optimization pipelines distill the judge into a model — see "synthetic judge" line.
