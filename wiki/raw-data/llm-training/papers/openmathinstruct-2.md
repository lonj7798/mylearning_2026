# OpenMathInstruct-2
- **Core Insight:** Switching the math-synthesis teacher from Mixtral to Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct and redoing the pipeline at ~8× scale (14M examples) closes most of the open-vs-closed gap on MATH and exposes clean data-scaling laws for synthetic CoT.
- **Guideline:** When building reasoning SFT, spending sampling budget on a frontier-grade open teacher (Llama-3.1-405B-class) yields steeper returns than stacking more data from a weaker teacher; scale the solutions-per-problem before scaling problem count.
- **Authors:** Shubham Toshniwal, Wei Du, Ivan Moshkov, Branislav Kisacanin, Alexan Ayrapetyan, Igor Gitman (Nvidia)
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.01560
- **Relevant topics:** math reasoning, synthetic CoT, scaling laws, teacher-model ablation

## Abstract
OpenMathInstruct-2 scales the OpenMathInstruct-1 pipeline to Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct teacher, generates 14M solutions, and publishes controlled ablations isolating (a) teacher strength, (b) solution-format, (c) problem-set composition, and (d) per-problem sample count. The resulting open dataset lets OpenMath2-Llama3.1-8B hit **67.8% MATH / 91.7% GSM8K**, within a point of closed-teacher distillations of the same size.

## Key Contributions
- **14M math solutions**, Apache-2.0 licensed.
- Ablation: **stronger teacher > more data from weaker teacher** — Llama-3.1-405B at 1M samples beats Mixtral at 10M.
- Ablation: **question augmentation** (paraphrase + novel question generation from source problems) adds +4 MATH points.
- Ablation: **solution-format study** — chosen format is CoT with minimal code usage (unlike OMI-1 which mixed heavy tool-integration).
- Open release of data + OpenMath2 models (1.5B/8B/70B).

## Synthesis pipeline (REQUIRED — concrete, modality-specific)
- **Seed input:** MATH train (7.5K) + GSM8K train (7.5K), **augmented** into ~600K problems via two operations:
  1. **Question paraphrasing** (teacher rewrites each problem).
  2. **Novel question generation** — teacher generates new problems in the style of the seed, grounded on topic/difficulty tags.
- **Generation step:** Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct samples K ≈ 32 CoT solutions per augmented problem, temperature 1.0, top-p 0.95.
- **Filtering:**
  - Extract final boxed answer via regex.
  - Gold-answer match using SymPy canonicalization (for MATH) or numeric-string match (GSM8K).
  - Near-duplicate suppression within a problem's accepted solutions.
- **Output shape:** 14M (problem, solution) pairs; ~23 solutions per augmented problem on average; trace length 300–1200 tokens typical.
- **Teacher:** Llama-3.1-405B-Instruct (BF16 inference, served via vLLM).
- **Cost / compute:** ~650K H100-hours for teacher sampling.

## Modality-specific technical details (REQUIRED — reasoning-trace)
- **Reasoning length distribution:** avg ~700 tokens; OMI-2 chose text-CoT with only occasional code — authors found pure text outperforms TIR at 405B scale.
- **Trace style:** standard CoT (non-reflective, short-CoT) — not long-CoT / R1-style.
- **Correctness verifier:** SymPy-based symbolic equivalence (MATH) + exact numeric match (GSM8K).
- **Question-augmentation prompts:** authors publish the exact paraphrase and generation prompts; key trick is conditioning on the topic tag extracted from MATH (e.g., "Algebra, Level 5").
- **Error mode:** false-positive rate (right answer, wrong reasoning) measured at ~7% on a human-audited sample.

## Quality / diversity evaluation
- OpenMath2-Llama3.1-8B: **91.7 GSM8K, 67.8 MATH** — SOTA among open 8B math models at release.
- Scaling laws: downstream MATH accuracy ≈ linear in log(dataset size) up to ~5M; flat beyond.
- Teacher ablation: Llama-3.1-405B > Llama-3.1-70B > Mixtral on per-example utility.

## Risks + gotchas
- **Question-aug contamination:** novel questions generated by the teacher may overlap with MATH/GSM8K test sets — authors run decontamination against test sets but 405B teacher may still leak.
- **Short-CoT ceiling:** the dataset is non-reflective CoT; students trained on it do not acquire backtracking. Separate long-CoT sources (s1, LIMO, R1 distills) needed for o1-style behavior.
- Compute cost out of reach for most labs (~0.6M H100-hours for teacher alone).
