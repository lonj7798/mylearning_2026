# Tulu 3 SFT Mixture
- **Core Insight:** Tulu 3's SFT stage is a curated prompt mixture, not a single dataset. Ai2 reports 939,344 prompts total, with 57% sourced from public resources and 43% synthetically generated in-house.
- **Guideline:** Build skill-specific submixes first, keep the ones that move the hardest skills, then merge, decontaminate, and downsample until the final mixture is balanced.
- **Authors:** Nathan Lambert, Jacob Morrison, Valentina Pyatkin, Shengyi Huang, Hamish Ivison, Faeze Brahman, et al.
- **Year:** 2024
- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/tulu-3-sft-mixture ; https://allenai.org/blog/tulu-3-technical ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.15124
- **Relevant topics:** SFT mixture design, synthetic prompts, decontamination, instruction tuning, open post-training

## Abstract
The public Tulu 3 dataset card exposes the exact SFT mixture used for the Tulu 3 instruct models: 939,344 prompt-completion examples drawn from 18 component sets. Ai2's blog says the recipe mixes public data with in-house synthetic generation, uses persona-driven synthesis to fill gaps in instruction following, math, coding, and safety, and iteratively rebalances the mix after decontamination against the eval suite. The SFT stage feeds later DPO and RLVR stages.

## Mix Composition
### Public and imported sources
- CoCoNot, 10,983 prompts.
- FLAN v2 via `ai2-adapt-dev/flan_v2_converted`, 89,982 prompts.
- No Robots, 9,500 prompts.
- OpenAssistant Guanaco, 7,132 prompts.
- Aya, 100,000 prompts.
- WildChat GPT-4, 100,000 prompts.
- TableGPT, 5,000 prompts.
- SciRIFF, 10,000 prompts.
- NuminaMath-TIR, 64,312 prompts.
- WildGuardMix, 50,000 prompts.
- Evol CodeAlpaca, 107,276 prompts.

### In-house synthetic family
- Tulu 3 Persona MATH, 149,960 prompts.
- Tulu 3 Persona GSM, 49,980 prompts.
- Tulu 3 Persona Python, 34,999 prompts.
- Tulu 3 Persona Algebra, 20,000 prompts.
- Tulu 3 Persona IF, 29,980 prompts.
- Tulu 3 WildJailbreak, 50,000 prompts.
- Tulu 3 Hardcoded, 240 prompts.

### How to read the split
- The 57/43 public-vs-in-house split is an aggregate statement from Ai2's blog.
- The per-source grouping above follows the dataset card and the provenance implied by the source names; Ai2 does not publish a separate row-level public/in-house label for each component in the prose. That grouping is therefore a best-effort reading, not a separately documented table.

## Technical Details
### How the mix was built
- Ai2 starts with public datasets that have clear provenance and licenses, then manually reviews each candidate source for diversity, hard-skill coverage, and decontamination.
- The team builds skill-specific data mixtures and models first, keeps the mixes that perform best on individual skills, and then combines them into a preview mix.
- The final mix is iteratively adjusted by adding and removing datasets, decontaminating against the evaluation suite, and downsampling oversized sources.
- The blog says all new synthetic SFT datasets use responses generated by GPT-4o or Claude 3.5 Sonnet for coding.

### Practical training lessons
- Reasoning, coding, instruction following, and safety need different prompt sources; do not collapse them into one undifferentiated instruction pool.
- Synthetic data is used to fill capability gaps, not to replace the public corpus.
- Decontamination is part of the data recipe, not an afterthought; Ai2 explicitly removes overlap with more than 2% of the eval suite.
- The SFT mix is designed to support later DPO and RLVR stages, so its job is broad skill coverage rather than single-task specialization.

## Risks + gotchas
- **Provenance complexity:** the mixture blends public, imported synthetic, and in-house synthetic sources with different licenses.
- **Aggregation ambiguity:** the public blog gives the 57/43 split at the total level, but not a per-source binary label in prose.
- **Synthetic bias:** the in-house family is generated by strong proprietary models, so style and policy behavior can bleed into the student.
