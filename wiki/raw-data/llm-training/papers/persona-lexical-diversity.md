## Abstract

The study analyzes human and synthetic instructions and persona-conditioned generation, including length-sensitive comparisons.

## Key Contributions

- Result (single study): More detailed personas do not automatically yield proportionately more lexical diversity.
- Interpretation: Control response length when comparing persona-conditioned diversity.
