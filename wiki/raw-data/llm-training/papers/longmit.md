# What are the Essential Factors in Crafting Effective Long Context Multi-Hop Instruction Datasets? Insights and Best Practices
- **Core Insight:** Long-context instruction data generated directly with Self-Instruct by Qwen2-72B is under 35% multi-hop and over 40% poor quality; routing generation through four agents (quality verification, single-hop question generation, multiple question sampling, multi-hop question merging) raises all three measured properties above 85% and lifts long-context QA accuracy by at least 7.54% on average (Abstract, §1, §4.2).
- **Guideline:** When synthesizing long-context instruction data, generate single-hop question-answer pairs per document first and merge related pairs into multi-hop questions afterwards, and filter with a scoring verifier rather than a binary high/low classifier, because scoring keeps discriminative power at long input lengths while classification labels nearly every long sample high-quality (§3.1.1, Appendix B).
- **Authors:** Zhi Chen, Qiguang Chen, Libo Qin, Qipeng Guo, Haijun Lv, Yicheng Zou, et al. (Shanghai AI Laboratory; Harbin Institute of Technology; Central South University)
- **Year:** 2024 (arXiv v1 2024-09; this card checked against v2, 2025-05-19)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01893 (code and data: https://github.com/WowCZ/LongMIT)
- **Source type:** paper
- **Relevant topics:** long-context instruction tuning, multi-hop question synthesis, multi-agent data generation, LLM-as-verifier at long input lengths, data quality metrics

## Abstract
The paper asks which factors determine the quality of long-context multi-hop instruction data. Preliminary manual annotation showed that fewer than 35% of samples generated by Qwen2-72B under Self-Instruct are multi-hop and over 40% are poor quality (Abstract). The authors propose MIMG (Multi-agent Interactive Multi-hop Generation), which combines a quality verification agent, a single-hop question generation agent, a multiple question sampling strategy, and a multi-hop question merger agent. They analyze document selection, question merging and validation techniques across 17 strategies, and release the resulting dataset, LongMIT. Models instruction-tuned on LongMIT outperform models tuned on larger human-annotated datasets (§1, §4.2).

## Key Contributions
- Quantifies the failure mode of Self-Instruct long-context data: under 35% multi-hop, high-quality examples only 60%, and over 45% of samples semantically duplicated (§1).
- Introduces the four-agent MIMG pipeline and reports over 85% multi-hop, high-quality, non-duplicative samples (§1, Table 2).
- Establishes that at long input lengths an LLM is a good selector but a poor annotator: near-perfect precision but low agreement (kappa) with human annotators (§3.1.1).
- Releases LongMIT: 64.40k samples, 5.07B tokens, 9 source domains, 2 languages, single-turn multi-hop long-document QA (Table 3, Appendix C.2-C.3).
- Reports instruction-tuning results on LongBench, RULER, IFEval, and ArenaHard, including short-context instruction following (§4.2, Tables 1, 4, 6, 8).

## Technical Details
### What LongMIT is
- LongMIT is **single-turn** long-document multi-hop question-answering data. A sample is one long context plus one merged multi-hop question and its answer, and the answer contains a reasoning path (Appendix C.2).
- Size: **64.40k samples, 5.07B tokens**, against LongAlign 9.89k / 0.17B, LongAlpaca 12.00k / 0.11B, ChatQA2 128.00k / 1.22B, NQ 315.20k / 4.56B (Table 3).
- Documents come from pre-trained corpora to prevent data leakage, spanning books, arXiv and CNKI papers, finance (ChatGLM-fin), Baike-Wiki and Pile-Wikipedia, Kepuchina and ScienceDaily, Pile-FreeLaw, Pile-PubMed Central, Pile-StackExchange, and Pile-OpenWebText2, in Chinese and English (Appendix C.2, Figure 9a).
- Sample context lengths span 0-128K, and hop counts span 1 to 4 (Figure 9b, 9c).

### The four agents
1. **Quality Verification Agent (QVA, §2.1).** Prompts an LLM to assign a 0-10 quality score and keeps samples above a threshold. Implementation uses **InternLM2-20B** as the verifier backbone with threshold **8.5**, chosen as the value with highest precision on an internally annotated 200-item verification set (Appendix C.4.2, A.3.2).
2. **Single-hop Question Generation Agent (SQGA, §2.2).** Generates multiple single-hop question-answer pairs per document. The selected strategy is **question-then-answer** generation with rationales, one question per query (Appendix D.2).
3. **Multiple Question Sampling (MQS, §2.3).** Builds a question-semantic relevance matrix over documents and samples related questions, either **intra-document** or **inter-document**. Implementation uses **BGE embeddings** for retrieval (Appendix D.2).
4. **Multi-hop Question Merger Agent (MQMA, §2.4).** Merges the sampled single-hop pairs into one multi-hop question and answer.

### Measured design choices
- **Scoring beats binary classification** as a verification strategy: scoring gives higher kappa and precision against human annotation (§3.1.1, Figure 3a-b). Kappa for scoring exceeds 0.50 and precision reaches **96.43%** (Appendix A.3.1). The stated reason is that with inputs averaging about 3K tokens the classifier labels nearly every long sample "high-quality", losing discriminative power (Appendix B).
- **LLMs are selectors, not annotators, at long context:** low kappa with humans but near-perfect precision (§3.1.1).
- **More scoring criteria reduce long-context bias:** with fewer than three criteria, improvements are limited and models overestimate irrelevant samples (§3.1.2, Figure 4a).
- **Guidelines are unnecessary** for advanced verifiers (§3.1.2, Figure 4b).
- **Rationale helps verification but not merging:** without rationale, verification performance drops by more than **8.6%** across domains, and with rationale the fluctuation is limited to **1.8%** (§3.1.2); in multi-hop merging, rationales fail to improve coherence and should be avoided (§3.4.2).
- **Question-answer pairs alone are sufficient for merging;** adding the long documents does not consistently improve merging and increases input tokens (§3.4.2).
- **Cost:** MIMG's agent interactions add about **3K input tokens** per sample, roughly 2.5 times, against documents averaging about **70K tokens**, and under **0.5K** extra output tokens (§4.1).

### Training setup used for the instruction-tuning results
- **64 A800 80G GPUs**, DeepSpeed + ZeRO-1, maximum sequence length set from **4K to 128K** with right truncation, Adam with learning rate **3 × 10⁻⁵**, β₁ = 0.9, β₂ = 0.95, packing plus FlashAttention, global batch **4M tokens**, **1 epoch** (Appendix E.1).

### Results
- **Data quality (Table 2, three independent manual evaluations):** LongMIT high-quality **94.8**, diversity **88.2**, multi-hop **94.8**; LongAlign 87.7 / 83.8 / 52.6; LongAlpaca 70.3 / 87.9 / 50.7; ChatQA2 83.1 / 80.3 / 30.4; NQ 90.2 / 63.2 / 10.3.
- **LongBench average, GPT-4o scored (Table 1):** InternLM2-1.8B +LongMIT **50.60** against +LongAlign 43.06; LLaMA3-8B +LongMIT **64.29** against +LongAlign 52.92; InternLM2-7B +LongMIT **67.48** against +LongAlign 57.71. The paper states an average gain of at least **7.54%** (§4.2), with 2WikiMQA, MuSiQue, and HotpotQA showing the larger improvements.
- **RULER, InternLM-2.5-7B-Enhance extended to 128K (Table 4):** average 47.56 → **70.27** at 128K with LongMIT; 92.78 → 93.48 at 4K.
- **Short-context instruction following:** IFEval inst_level_strict_acc for Llama-3-8B-ProLong 0.1199 → **0.1463** with LongMIT, against 0.0132 with ChatQA2 (Table 6). ArenaHard: ProLong 7.2, +LongMIT 6.7, +ChatQA2 4.4 (Table 8).
- **Component ablation (Table 5, high-quality / diversity / multi-hop):** MIMG 94.8 / 88.2 / 94.8; w/o MQMA 85.0 / 85.7 / **45.7**; w/o MQS 92.8 / **65.4** / 89.4; w/o SQGA 82.4 / 69.4 / 73.2; w/o QVA **78.3** / 88.1 / 91.0.
- **Hop scaling:** holding training volume constant, accuracy rises from 1-hop to 2-hop to 4-hop, where 1-hop is a reproduction of the Quest dataset (§4.3.2, Figure 11).

## Findings relevant to generality
- Training on LongMIT improved the short-context instruction-following benchmark IFEval above the base model, while ChatQA2 training caused a pronounced decline there (Appendix D.5, Table 6). On ArenaHard the LongMIT model stays below the base model (7.2 → 6.7) but well above ChatQA2 (4.4) (Table 8).
- The paper reports that the tuned model generalizes to single-hop tasks not seen during instruction tuning (§4.2, Appendix F).
- Dataset-level attribution (Appendix A.2): high-quality annotation drives foundational long-context comprehension, diversity drives breadth across fields, and the multi-hop property drives complex reasoning tasks such as HotpotQA and MuSiQue.
