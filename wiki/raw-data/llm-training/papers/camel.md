# CAMEL: Communicative Agents for "Mind" Exploration of Large Language Models
- **Core Insight:** Two LLM agents assigned distinct roles ("AI User" giving tasks, "AI Assistant" solving them) and prompted under an **Inception-prompting** scheme produce genuinely collaborative, goal-directed multi-turn dialogues whose content is richer and more task-oriented than undirected self-chat — the role-specification is what unlocks diversity.
- **Guideline:** For role-play / task-oriented multi-turn synthesis, assign distinct agent personas via Inception prompting (each agent told who they are, who the other is, and the task protocol), and let them converge on a task solution; cap turns and use task-completion as a termination signal.
- **Authors:** Guohao Li, Hasan Abed Al Kader Hammoud, Hani Itani, Dmitrii Khizbullin, Bernard Ghanem (KAUST)
- **Year:** 2023
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17760
- **Relevant topics:** role-play, multi-agent, Inception prompting, CAMEL

## Abstract
CAMEL introduces **Inception Prompting** — a system-prompt pattern where two LLM agents are initialized with detailed role descriptions (AI User who specifies tasks; AI Assistant who solves them) and a task-completion protocol. Running this protocol across 50 assistant roles × 50 user roles × 20 topic domains yields 25K goal-directed conversations. CAMEL also released a 1M-instance AI Society dataset. The framework's Inception-prompting idea is widely reused in 2024/2025 agentic data pipelines (APIGen-MT, AgentInstruct).

## Key Contributions
- **Inception Prompting** — a reproducible role-play protocol.
- **AI Society dataset** — ~1M role-play instruction pairs covering 50×50 role combinations.
- **Code / Math / Science** datasets generated by the same protocol with domain-specialized personas.
- Released frameworks (camel-ai/camel) widely used in multi-agent research.

## Synthesis pipeline (REQUIRED — concrete, modality-specific)

### Inception Prompting
- **Shared context:** a task specification (high-level goal).
- **AI User prompt** defines: your role, the assistant's role, the task, and a protocol:
  - "Never forget you are a <user-role> and I am a <assistant-role>."
  - "Give me one instruction at a time."
  - "When the task is solved, say `<CAMEL_TASK_DONE>`."
- **AI Assistant prompt** is the mirror, instructing solution-provision behavior.
- Both prompts enforce strict turn protocol: user always gives next instruction; assistant always responds with solution.

### Role combinatorics
- **50 assistant roles:** Accountant, Architect, Astronaut, Biologist, … (domain experts).
- **50 user roles:** Entrepreneur, Graduate Student, Journalist, … (information-seeker personas).
- **20 topic domains:** per dataset variant (Society, Code, Math, Science).
- Total combinations drive dataset size.

### Dialog generation
- Two separate GPT-3.5 calls alternate per turn.
- Continues until `<CAMEL_TASK_DONE>` or turn cap (~20).

### Filtering
- Dialog must contain `<CAMEL_TASK_DONE>` or reach a natural conclusion.
- Length checks (min 4 turns).
- Coherence check.

- **Output shape:** AI Society ~1M dialogs, avg 6–10 turns. Other CAMEL datasets (Code, Math) ~50K–100K each.
- **Teacher model:** GPT-3.5-Turbo.
- **Cost:** ~$5K–10K in API at 2023 rates.

## Modality-specific technical details (REQUIRED — conversation)
- **Turn-count distribution:** median 6–8, tail to 20.
- **Speaker-role protocol:** two separate GPT-3.5 calls, each with a distinct Inception prompt.
- **Persona conditioning:** explicit — role-name in both prompts.
- **Safety post-filter:** inherits GPT-3.5's safety layer.
- **Topic coverage:** 50×50×20 combinatorial — much broader than Baize's single-seed, narrower stylistically than UltraChat's sector taxonomy.
- **Task-completion protocol** is the novel element — turns end when task is solved, not at a fixed turn budget.

## Quality / diversity evaluation
- CAMEL-AI Society diversity: higher than Baize on lexical diversity, comparable to UltraChat.
- Downstream SFT on CAMEL data: models fine-tuned on CAMEL-Math exceed baseline on GSM8K.
- Inception-prompting pattern adopted in later datasets (Airoboros, OpenHermes-2.5).

## Risks + gotchas
- **Role-play artifacts:** both agents introduce themselves in every turn ("As an accountant,…"); downstream model must learn to ignore these formulaic phrases.
- **GPT-3.5 ceiling:** 2023-era teacher means content quality caps below modern models.
- **Protocol rigidity:** `<CAMEL_TASK_DONE>` formulation leaks into student models.
- **50×50 coverage does not ensure quality** — many role pairs yield low-quality dialogs (e.g., Astronaut × Bartender).
