This catalog collects the papers discussed during the chapter-23 reading session. Source cards record verification scope and limitations; abstract-only entries are not full recipe extractions. Publication year can differ from first arXiv release.

## Issue map

| Issue | Useful evidence | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Rare facts / lost probability support | Knowledge frequency, grounded synthesis, mixed-data scaling | Rare human interaction coverage |
| Unusual conversation sequences | User simulation, skeletons, successful trajectories, failure rubrics | Population frequency or arbitrary new-skill success |
| Tone / wording / emotion | Human messages, lexical controls, emotional scaffolds | Behavioral correctness or acoustic realism |
| Prosody / correction / turn taking | Human spoken benchmarks, audio preferences, expressive TTS | Factual verification or all accents/conditions |

## Evidence boundary

Result (single study) reports a source-specific finding; Interpretation is a proposed connection; Open question marks unverified transfer. No listed paper provides a complete dynamic-skill sales long-tail recipe or a universal mixture ratio. Rarity requires a defined population and frequency; diversity, difficulty, and novelty are separate.
