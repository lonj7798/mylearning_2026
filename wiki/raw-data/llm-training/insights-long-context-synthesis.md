# Insights — Long-context and long-conversation synthesis

- Extending the window does not give the long-context behaviour; a long-context SFT stage does. With 76k ShareGPT examples held fixed, adding 10k synthetic 8k-64k instruction examples is what moves long-context scores ([[longalign]]); 1.1M + 300K synthesized long-context QA examples plus 150K short QA raise Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2 on long-context tasks while keeping short-context ability ([[in2-film]]); 1.6k instruction-answer pairs whose contexts were written by GPT-4o-mini (1 relevant + 9 irrelevant contexts) raise the 8-task LongBench average on LLaMA3.1-8B ([[context-synthesis-short-to-long]]). **Replicated** across three pipelines.
- Naive self-instruct synthesis over long documents produces low-quality supervision. Long-context instruction data generated directly with Self-Instruct by Qwen2-72B is under 35% multi-hop and over 40% poor quality; routing generation through four agents including quality verification fixes both ([[longmit]]). Hierarchical generation from 4K/12K-token chunks scales the same idea to 1M-token dialogues at 62.95% RULER accuracy ([[hierarchical-million-token-synth]]).
- Preference and RL data can also be synthesized short-to-long. 45K self-generated short-to-long preference samples raise Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2's InfiniteBench average from 13.82 to 39.27 while MMLU stays near its starting value ([[longpo]]); GRPO on 21,024 examples whose hardest part is 7,500 KeyChain items padded to about 16K tokens is the RL counterpart ([[loongrl]]).
- Synthetic needle tasks overstate long-context ability relative to realistic tasks. Across 35 instruction-tuned models at 128K tokens, Spearman correlation with ∞Bench QA is 0.63 for NIAH, 0.81 for RULER multi-key, and 0.88 for the RAG task HotpotQA ([[helmet]]); of 17 models claiming 32K or more, only half stay above a fixed quality threshold at 32K ([[ruler]]); with the question sharing almost no words with the needle, 11 of 13 models claiming 128K score at or below half their short-context result ([[nolima]], [[chroma-context-rot]], [[context-length-alone-hurts]]). **Replicated** across four independent evaluations.
- Position in the prompt and retrieval quality are confounders in any long-context measurement. With 20 retrieved documents GPT-3.5-Turbo scores 75.8% when the answer document is first and 53.8% when it is tenth ([[lost-in-the-middle]]); on a fixed 100K-document corpus the same agent scores 55.90% with BM25 and 70.12% with a dense retriever ([[browsecomp-plus]]); fewer than 5% of attention heads act as retrieval heads across four model families ([[retrieval-head]]).
- Long conversations are a separate problem from long documents, and the library measures the gap. Answering from only the evidence sessions GPT-4o scores 0.870 on LongMemEval, and 0.606 when reading the full ~115K-token history ([[longmemeval]]); on LoCoMo humans score 87.9 F1 against 41.4 for the best RAG setting ([[locomo]]); on BEAM, probing scores fall from 0.239-0.280 at 100K tokens to 0.104-0.133 at 10M ([[beam-10m-conversations]]); all six frontier models tested score below 50% on 273 multi-turn conversations ([[multichallenge]]). Synthesis references for this regime are [[conversation-chronicles]], [[ultrachat-pipeline]], [[baize-construction]], [[camel]], and [[soda]].
