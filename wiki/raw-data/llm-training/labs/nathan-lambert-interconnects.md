## Position in the field
Interconnects is the single most consulted external commentary layer in the open post-training community. Lambert's dual role — researcher at Ai2 (lead author on Tulu 3) and blog author — means the posts carry both primary-source authority on Ai2's releases and timely secondary analysis of everyone else's. His angle is explicitly opinionated: he rates which tricks will propagate, which results will replicate, and which hyperparameters are actually doing the work.

Key recurring themes in the blog: (1) DPO vs PPO tradeoffs, (2) the case for RLVR over RLHF, (3) open-vs-closed post-training capability gap, (4) why GRPO's length-normalization matters, (5) running commentary on Chinese open releases (DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi).

The blog has effectively become a canonical reference for understanding *why* pipelines look the way they do — not just what the numbers are. Several papers cite Interconnects posts directly.
