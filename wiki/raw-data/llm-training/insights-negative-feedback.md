# Insights — Negative samples and negative feedback

- "Negative" names four different mechanisms and the library keeps them separate: a sample with negative marginal value that a filter removes ([[rejection-sampling-finetuning]], [[alpagasus]]); a failure used as content in the input or in a corrected target ([[self-correct-rl]], [[orca-2]]); a failure trained under a control token; and an explicit decrease of a sample's likelihood ([[dpo]], [[verl-ppo-loss]]). Only the fourth removes probability mass from the sample, and only the fourth carries likelihood-displacement risk.
- Training only on chosen responses does not hold the rejected response down. When OPT-350M is fine-tuned with cross-entropy on chosen HH-RLHF responses only, the log-probability of rejected responses rises together with that of chosen responses, which is the measurement ORPO's odds-ratio term is introduced to fix ([[orpo]]).
- An explicit push-down moves probability mass to whatever is next most likely, not to the intended target. DPO on on-policy refusal pairs lowered the training-set refusal rate of Llama-3-8B-Instruct from 74.4% to 33.4% and of Gemma-2B-IT from 80.5% to 54.8% ([[likelihood-displacement]]); when the empirical preference probability reaches 0 or 1 the DPO optimum assigns probability 0 to the dispreferred action for any β, which is the failure IPO's objective removes ([[ipo]]). **Replicated** as a mechanism, one empirical and one analytical.
- Anchoring the negative term with a positive likelihood term is the repair that several sources report. Plain DPO on correct-vs-incorrect chain-of-thought pairs gives 61.8% on GSM8K, below the 63.5% of SFT on gold CoTs; adding an NLL term on the chosen CoT recovers and exceeds it ([[rpo]]); OpenRLHF exposes the same NLL-on-chosen option in its DPO trainer ([[openrlhf-dpo]]); verl's dual clip caps the loss contribution of each negative-advantage token ([[verl-ppo-loss]]). Off-policy REINFORCE with advantage r − V keeps a wide support only while V stays below the behaviour policy's expected reward ([[asymmetric-reinforce]]).
- Failures carry usable signal when they are paired with a matched success or localized to a step. Keeping the incorrect solutions a self-improvement loop generates and training a DPO verifier on correct/incorrect pairs gives 6 to 17 absolute points over STaR-style baselines ([[v-star]]); pairing expert-success with the model's own failure trajectories reaches average reward 67.4 on WebShop ([[eto-trial-and-error]]); using one reasoning step as the preference unit raises MATH by about 3 points with 10K pairs on models above 70B ([[step-dpo]], [[math-shepherd]], [[omegaprm]]); rewriting agent data with two types of negative samples raises the Llama2-7B agent score to 41.7 from 38.2 ([[agent-flan]]).
- Most released trajectory data is failure data, so the discard-versus-use decision is a large one. The Terminal-Bench trajectory set contains 52,104 trials at a 39.6% pass rate, leaving about 60% failed trajectories with binary outcome labels ([[terminal-bench-trajectories]]). The library has no card yet for the negative-aware fine-tuning line that measures how much of the gain negatives contribute; see the gap log in [[insights]].
