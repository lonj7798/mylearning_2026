# Qwen3 Strong-to-Weak Distillation (Qwen3 Technical Report, 2025)

- **Core Insight:** At industrial scale, the smaller Qwen3 models are trained not by rerunning full RL but by **distilling from a stronger Qwen3 teacher** — combining off-policy distillation (train on teacher outputs) with **on-policy distillation** (student samples sequences, minimize KL to the teacher's token distributions) — reaching higher benchmark scores at roughly one-tenth the RL compute.
- **Guideline:** For a family of models, spend RL compute on the largest/strongest model, then use strong-to-weak (largely on-policy) distillation to bring the smaller siblings up — it is far cheaper than independent RL per model and often scores higher.
- **Source:** Qwen Team, "Qwen3 Technical Report," 2025, arXiv:2505.09388. **Thesis extracted, not verbatim** — primary-source fetch was blocked by a monthly-spend limit on 2026-07-06. The headline numbers below are as reported in the report's distillation table (Table 21) and reproduced by [[tm-on-policy-distillation]], which cites them directly.

| Method | AIME'24 | GPU-hours |
|---|---|---|

| + RL | 67.6% | 17,920 |

→ **~10× less compute than RL, at a higher score.** This is the single most-cited industrial data point for on-policy distillation and the empirical backbone of the economics chapter.

## The two-stage distillation pipeline (thesis)

The report describes strong-to-weak distillation for the lightweight models in roughly two stages:
1. **Off-policy distillation** — the student is trained on outputs generated by the teacher across a broad prompt set (the sequence-level KD move, [[kim-rush-seqkd]]).
2. **On-policy distillation** — the student then generates its own responses and is trained to **minimize the KL divergence to the teacher's logits** on those self-generated sequences (the mechanism of [[tm-on-policy-distillation]] / [[agarwal-gkd]]).

The combination transfers both the teacher's outputs and its *per-token judgment on the student's own behavior*, which is why it recovers RL-level (and better) capability at a fraction of the cost.

## Why it matters for the course
This is the "does it actually work at scale?" evidence: on-policy distillation is not a lab curiosity but the production mechanism a frontier lab uses to build its smaller models. It anchors ch-05's trade-off ("~10× cheaper than RL") and gives the capstone a real precedent for distilling a strong teacher (Claude / large Qwen) into a smaller deployed seller model.
