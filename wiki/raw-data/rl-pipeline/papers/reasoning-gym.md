# Reasoning Gym: Reasoning Environments for Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards
- **Core Insight:** Procedural generators remove the reference-answer problem: each of 102 datasets in 10 categories (Table 6) generates unlimited items with an answer checked by an algorithm (principle P1), and difficulty is a generator parameter (P3); a curriculum that raises the level after 70% accuracy over 20 steps beat fixed-difficulty training at every listed level (e.g., Spell Backwards length 4: 30.00 → 70.67, Table 5).
- **Guideline:** When a task family can be generated by a program, train on a generator with a difficulty parameter instead of a fixed dataset, and advance difficulty when policy accuracy crosses a threshold (the paper used 70% over 20 steps), because the curriculum runs beat fixed-level runs on every reported level (§5, Table 5); expect transfer across categories to be uneven, since RG-Algorithmic training raised Algebra by +29.1 but RG-Algebra training lowered ARC by −2.3 (Table 2).

## Summary
Reasoning Gym provides generators and verifiers for algebra, algorithms, arithmetic, cognition (including ARC), code, games, geometry, graphs, induction and logic. Each generator exposes difficulty parameters (e.g., graph node count, polynomial degree), structural parameters (dimensionality, proof depth) and stylistic parameters (presentation only) (§2). The paper evaluates frontier models zero-shot and trains Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct with GRPO on subsets.

## Key Contributions
- 102 datasets in 10 categories (Table 6) with verifiers that need no human judgment (P1).
- Parametric difficulty control (P3) and a "difficulty cliff" measurement: o3-mini drops 71.9% on code and 33.8% on graphs from easy to hard configs (§3.2, Fig. 3b).
- Intra-domain, cross-domain and external-benchmark transfer results from GRPO training (Tables 1–4).
- Curriculum vs fixed-difficulty comparison (§5, Table 5).

## Technical Details
**Reward (§4, Fig. 5 caption).** Accuracy reward 1.0 plus format reward 0.2.

**Training.** Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct, GRPO, about 1,500 A6000 hours (§4).

**Transfer results.**
- Intra-domain Acc@3 (Table 1): Algebra 5.0 → 16.7; Algorithmic 52.3 → 59.7; Games 0.0 → 3.3.
- Cross-domain (Table 2): RG-Algorithmic raises Algebra 23.83 → 52.89 and Geometry 0.83 → 23.17; RG-Logic raises Cognition 11.62 → 24.94; RG-Algebra lowers ARC 6.49 → 4.18.
- External (Table 3), RG-Math: GSM8K 76.2 → 76.7; MATH 48.5 → 58.2; Big-Bench Hard 8.68 → 16.34.
- MMLU-Pro (Table 4), RG-Math: Math 54.63 → 60.25; Physics 38.49 → 44.19.

**Curriculum (§5, Table 5).** Advance one level after exceeding 70% over 20 steps. Mini Sudoku (8–10 empty cells) 6.67 → 20.00; Count Primes (100–500) 4.00 → 30.67. The authors note curriculum value may be limited where the progression path is ambiguous.
