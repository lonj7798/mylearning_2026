## Core strengths

### Framework installation
Learner internalises structural frameworks (multi-stage loops, taxonomies, corollary sets) reliably in a single read cycle. After ch-18, the six-stage synthetic-data loop was correctly applied to a novel domain without any teacher scaffolding.

### Filter vs Verify distinction
Demonstrated sharp conceptual precision in qa.md Q2: correctly identified the axis as "surface form vs ground truth," not "non-LLM vs LLM." Used code examples (AST parse vs unit tests) to anchor the distinction.

### Anchor conceptual depth
qa.md Q7 correctly distinguished anchor ⊇ seed and identified Job B (calibration) as the load-bearing role — a subtlety many learners collapse to "anchor = seed."

## Transfer skills

### Novel domain application (self-initiated)
In ch-18 discuss, learner self-extended the six-stage checklist to a sales-call conversation dataset without prompting. All six stages were addressed with domain-appropriate reasoning:
- Stage 2: compliance-bound keyword filters (correct for regulated domain)
- Stage 3: correctly identified multi-turn dedup as a harder open problem
- Stage 4: LLM-judge for the right reason (no symbolic verifier for sales quality)
- Stage 5: diversity selection for objection-handling coverage
- Stage 6: replay-as-anchor mixing with correct rationale (accumulation over replacement)

### Career-frame integration
Immediately mapped the "verification is the moat" career advice to their own domain: articulated that judge calibration, not generation, is the defensible advantage in sales-AI. Shows ability to connect abstract principles to concrete professional strategy.

## Metacognitive habits

### Non-defensive self-correction loop
When given direct corrections with severity tags (✓ ⚠ ✗), learner integrates all corrections in a single pass without defensiveness and immediately applies the corrected understanding. Observed in ch-18 discuss: four corrections on Alpaca stages → all integrated in one response → applied correctly in self-extension probe.

### Explicitly requests tighter feedback
Learner asked the teacher mid-session to flag imprecise terms in real time rather than at end of summary. This metacognitive self-awareness about their own vocabulary-substitution habit is a strong signal; learner knows where they drift and proactively requests guardrails.

### Q&A quality
Questions raised during reading (qa.md) are precise, causally framed, and non-trivial. Q3 (why Pipeline A destroys student models), Q10 (generator-verifier independence), Q12 (compound corollary) all show learner is reading for mechanism, not just definitions.
