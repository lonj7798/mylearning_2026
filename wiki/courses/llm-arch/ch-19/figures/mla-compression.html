Key insight: MLA caches a shared latent vector (dc = 512) plus a small RoPE key (dhR = 64), totaling 576 dims per token per layer. Standard MHA caches 2 × H × dk = 32,768 dims. The up-projection matrices WUK and WUV are static model weights — they are not cached. Each head reconstructs unique K/V from the shared latent, preserving representational diversity unlike MQA.
