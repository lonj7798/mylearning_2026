How information flows: Global layers (red) read the full context and write long-range information into the residual stream. The subsequent 5 local layers (blue) access that information not by attending to distant tokens directly, but by reading what the previous global layer deposited. Each global layer acts as a "refresh point" for long-range dependencies.
