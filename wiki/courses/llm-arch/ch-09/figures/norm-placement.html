Training Loss Curves (Simulated)

Post-Norm

Norm sits ON the residual path. Gradient magnitude: O(d * L). Requires warmup to avoid divergence.
x_l+1 = LN(x_l + f(x_l))

Pre-Norm

Norm is INSIDE the branch. Residual path is clean identity. Gradient: O(1). No warmup needed.
x_l+1 = x_l + f(LN(x_l))
