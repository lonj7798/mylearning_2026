Key properties: (1) Each dimension pair (2i, 2i+1) oscillates at a unique frequency, creating a unique "fingerprint" per position. (2) PE(pos+k) = R_k * PE(pos) where R_k is a rotation matrix -- relative position is a linear transformation. (3) The dot product between PE(pos) and PE(pos+k) decays with |k|, providing a built-in distance bias. (4) High-frequency dimensions distinguish nearby positions; low-frequency dimensions encode global position.
