Emergence vs. Metric Choice

Schaeffer et al. (2023) showed that "emergent abilities" can be created or destroyed by changing the evaluation metric. Left: exact-match accuracy shows a sharp jump. Right: per-token accuracy reveals smooth improvement.

Answer Length (tokens) 8
Scaling Steepness 3.0
Exact-Match Accuracy (Nonlinear Metric)
Per-Token Accuracy (Linear Metric)

The mechanism: Per-token accuracy p(scale) improves smoothly via a sigmoid. Exact-match accuracy for an L-token answer is p^L (all tokens must be correct). When L = 8 and p goes from 0.7 to 0.95, exact-match jumps from 0.7^8 = 0.058 to 0.95^8 = 0.663 -- an 11x increase that looks like sudden "emergence."

Try it: Set answer length to 1 -- both charts look the same (no emergence). Increase to 15+ -- the "phase transition" becomes even sharper. The emergence is in the metric, not the model.
