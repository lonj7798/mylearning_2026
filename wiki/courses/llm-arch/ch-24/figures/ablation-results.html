QK-Norm
Clear Win
RMSNorm applied to queries and keys before the dot product. Bounds attention logits to prevent softmax saturation during long training runs.
OLMo 1 (without)
Loss spikes during training, required restarts. Attention logits grew unboundedly.
OLMo 2 (with)
Stable training across 4-6T tokens. No loss spikes. Attention logits bounded.
Impact
Evidence: Loss curves with/without QK-norm. Direct comparison at 7B and 13B scales.
Z-Loss Regularization
Clear Win
Penalty on log-sum-exp of output logits: L_z = alpha * log^2(sum(exp(z_j))). Prevents logit explosion at the vocabulary projection layer.
Without Z-loss
Output logit magnitudes grow over training, risk of overflow in long runs.
With Z-loss
Output logits stay bounded. Complementary to QK-norm (covers output layer).
Impact
Evidence: Logit magnitude tracking during training. Combined effect with QK-norm.
RMSNorm (from nonparametric LayerNorm)
Clear Win
Replaced OLMo 1's nonparametric LayerNorm (no learnable parameters) with RMSNorm (learnable scale, no mean centering). Simpler, slightly better convergence.
Nonparametric LayerNorm
No learnable parameters. Fixed normalization with mean subtraction.
RMSNorm
Learnable scale. Drops mean centering (dispensable per ablation evidence).
Impact
Evidence: Controlled ablation. Consistent with broader literature (Llama, Mistral use RMSNorm).
RoPE (from absolute positional embeddings)
Clear Win
Replaced OLMo 1's absolute positional embeddings with Rotary Position Embeddings. Encodes relative position through rotation, enabling better generalization.
Absolute Pos Emb
Learned per-position vectors. Limited length generalization.
RoPE
Rotation-based relative encoding. Standard in all modern LLMs.
Impact
Evidence: Controlled ablation showing downstream task improvement. Aligns with community consensus.
Two-Stage Pre-Training
Clear Win
Stage 1 on broad web data, Stage 2 on curated Dolmino mix during LR annealing. Curated data during annealing has outsized impact on downstream tasks.
Stage 1 Only
Broad competence but weaker on reasoning, math, and instruction following.
Stage 1 + Stage 2
Significant gains on academic benchmarks. 7B outperforms Llama-3.1-8B.
Impact
Evidence: Stage 1 only vs Stage 1+2 comparison on OLMES suite (20 benchmarks).
Model Souping
Clear Win
Weight-averaging multiple Stage 2 variants (different data mixes) to produce the final model. Exploits linear mode connectivity from shared Stage 1 checkpoint.
Best Single Variant
Performance depends on which annealing mix happened to be best.
Souped Model
Consistent improvement. More robust than any single variant. Zero inference cost.
Impact
Evidence: Multiple variant comparison. Souped model outperforms best individual across benchmarks.
Post-Norm vs Pre-Norm Placement
Conditional
OLMo 2 uses post-norm (RMSNorm after attention/FFN). This only works stably because of QK-norm + Z-loss + scaled initialization. Pre-norm is safer without these.
Pre-norm (standard)
Always stable. Default for Llama, Mistral, etc. May sacrifice some representational capacity.
Post-norm + stabilizers
Works with QK-norm + Z-loss. Potentially better final quality. Fails without stabilizers.
Impact
Evidence: Post-norm succeeded only when combined with QK-norm + Z-loss. Not independently validated.
GQA vs MHA
Scale-Dependent
MHA (full heads) used at 7B and 13B. GQA (8 KV heads) adopted only at 32B for KV cache manageability. Not a quality-driven decision.
MHA (7B, 13B)
Full representational capacity. KV cache manageable at these scales.
GQA (32B)
5x KV cache reduction. Adopted for serving pragmatics, not quality improvement.
Quality
Efficiency
Evidence: GQA adopted at 32B for inference efficiency, not benchmarked against MHA at that scale.
Bias Term Removal
No Effect
All bias terms removed from linear layers (Q, K, V, O, FFN projections, output head). Pure y = Wx. RMSNorm makes per-layer biases redundant.
With bias (y = Wx + b)
Standard neural network convention. Adds d parameters per projection.
Without bias (y = Wx)
No measurable quality change. Simpler implementation, cleaner quantization.
Impact
Evidence: No measurable difference. Removed for simplicity. Consistent with Llama precedent.
