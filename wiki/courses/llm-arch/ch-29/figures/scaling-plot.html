Adjust the sliders to explore different scaling scenarios.
Chinchilla pattern: beta_exp > beta_base with alpha_exp < alpha_base = persistent diverging gap (experimental is better AND scales faster). This is the strongest evidence for an architecture claim.
Converging pattern: beta_exp ~ beta_base with alpha_exp < alpha_base = gap stays constant. The modification helps at all scales equally.
Crossing pattern: beta_exp < beta_base = baseline eventually wins at larger scale. Danger zone for architecture claims.
