Key relationships: PPL = exp(CE). Cross-entropy H(P,Q) = -log Q(correct token) when the true distribution is one-hot. KL divergence = H(P,Q) - H(P). Since H(P) = 0 for a one-hot target, CE = KL divergence in this case.
Intuition: Perplexity of k means the model is as uncertain as choosing uniformly among k tokens.
