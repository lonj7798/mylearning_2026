Scenario

The model predicts a distribution over 3 tokens. The correct token is token A. We visualize the loss surface as a function of two logit dimensions (the third is fixed at 0 for identifiability). The red ball follows gradient descent toward the minimum.

What you are seeing: The heatmap shows cross-entropy loss as a function of logits for tokens B and C (token A logit fixed at 0). The minimum is at (B=-inf, C=-inf), i.e., all probability on token A. With label smoothing, the minimum shifts: the model targets P(A)=1-eps, distributing eps across B and C.
Key insight: Without label smoothing, the gradient pulls logits toward -infinity (infinite confidence). Label smoothing creates a finite minimum, acting as implicit regularization.
