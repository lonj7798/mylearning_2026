Key Observations

1. A quantized large model beats a smaller model at full precision. 70B-INT4 outperforms 13B-FP16 on MMLU despite similar memory footprints, because quantization loses less information than removing 80% of parameters.

2. INT4 is the practical sweet spot. The jump from FP16 to INT4 doubles throughput with only 1-2 points MMLU drop. Going below INT4 (INT3) causes disproportionate quality loss.

3. Distillation shifts the frontier. Phi-4 (14B, distilled) sits on the Pareto frontier alongside much larger models, demonstrating that high-quality training data can substitute for parameter count.
