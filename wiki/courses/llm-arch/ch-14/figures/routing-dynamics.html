What you are seeing

Each bar chart shows the fraction of tokens routed to each expert at the current training step. The ideal distribution is 1/N per expert (uniform).

Left (No Balancing): Without intervention, 1-2 experts dominate quickly. By step 100, most experts receive near-zero tokens. This is routing collapse.

Center (Auxiliary Loss): The balancing loss (alpha * N * sum(f_i * P_i)) pulls the distribution toward uniform. Load is balanced but oscillates — the loss term competes with the main objective, causing the router to "fight" between specialization and uniformity.

Right (Bias Terms): DeepSeek-V3's approach adjusts bias terms outside of gradient descent. Load converges smoothly to near-uniform without oscillation, because the bias terms do not interfere with the training loss gradient.
