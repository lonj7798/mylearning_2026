Extrapolation Behavior Summary

Method How it encodes position At training length Beyond training length Why
Sinusoidal Fixed sin/cos added to embeddings Smooth, unique Defined but untested Frequencies continue, but model never trained on them
Learned Lookup table (one vector per position) Flexible, optimized Undefined (crash) No embedding exists for position > L. Hard wall.

Reading the Heatmaps

X-axis = sequence position (0 to seq_len). Y-axis = encoding dimension. Color intensity shows the encoding value (red = positive, blue = negative, black = zero). The dashed red line marks the training length boundary. Beyond it, watch for: Learned showing solid gray (undefined), RoPE high-frequency bands becoming chaotic, and ALiBi / iRoPE continuing smoothly. Sinusoidal continues its pattern but the model has no training signal for those positions.
