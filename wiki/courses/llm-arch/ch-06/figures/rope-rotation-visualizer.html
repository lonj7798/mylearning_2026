How RoPE Works

theta_j = 10000^(-2j/d) | Rotation angle for q at pos m: m * theta_j

Each pair of dimensions (2j, 2j+1) forms a 2D rotation plane. At position m, the query vector is rotated by angle m * theta_j in this plane. The key vector at position n is rotated by n * theta_j. Their dot product equals |q||k| * cos((m-n) * theta_j), which depends only on the relative position (m - n). Low j = high frequency (fast rotation, local position). High j = low frequency (slow rotation, global position).
