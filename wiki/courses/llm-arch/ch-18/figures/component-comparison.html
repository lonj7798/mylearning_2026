Key Architectural Insight

Three components are unchanged across all four Llama generations: GQA attention, pre-norm RMSNorm, and SwiGLU activation. These represent settled design choices. The variation has moved to higher-order structural decisions: dense vs MoE, position encoding strategy, multimodal integration method, and post-training methodology. Component convergence enables structural divergence.
