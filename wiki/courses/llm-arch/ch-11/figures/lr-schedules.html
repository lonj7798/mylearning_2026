Key observations:
Cosine decays gradually from the start, spending most of training at a moderate LR. Llama 3 (405B) used this with eta_max=8e-5 over 15T tokens.
WSD maintains peak LR for ~70% of training, then decays sharply. This maximizes the learning signal during the stable phase but requires knowing total steps in advance.
Linear Decay is the simplest schedule. It is rarely used alone for large runs but serves as a useful baseline for understanding the other two.

Practical note: The warmup phase (first 1-2%) is universal across all schedules. Without it, randomly initialized models receive extreme gradients that cause immediate divergence. OLMo 2's two-stage training transitions from Stage 1 to Stage 2 data during the annealing phase (right side of the chart), which is why the schedule choice interacts with curriculum design.
