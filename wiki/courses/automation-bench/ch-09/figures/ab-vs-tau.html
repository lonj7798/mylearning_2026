pass^k vs pass@k — Two Metric Philosophies
pass^k (reliability): all k trials must succeed — used by τ-bench.
pass@k (capability breadth): at least one of k trials must succeed — used by pass@1 leaderboards.
Per-trial success p = 0.75
pass^k = p^k (τ-bench reliability)
pass@k = 1 − (1−p)^k (capability breadth)
