Model: bf16 weights = 2 B/param, bf16 gradients = 2 B/param, AdamW states = 12 B/param (fp32 master + m + v). Under ZeRO-3 the sharded states (weights+grads+Adam for Full-FT) are divided by W; activations & overhead stay per-GPU (node-invariant). LoRA: gradients/Adam only over the <0.5% trainable adapter params (≈0 GB here); base 2P weights stay resident on every rank regardless of W. Units are decimal GB so weights/Adam reproduce the chapter's round 2P/12P figures (27B → 54 / 324 GB, Rule-of-16 = 432 GB). Activations use the illustrative form L·b·h·(34 + 5·a·s/h)·s bytes (fixed ~8B-class shape L=32, h=4096, a=32, b=1), scaled to the read.md anchor Llama-3-8B @ seq=32,768 ≈ 240 GB; values are teaching-scale, not a specific run. Overhead ≈ 9 GB (CUDA context + NCCL + ZeRO gather buffers). Bandwidth reference: A100 ≈ 2.0 TB/s, H100 ≈ 3.35 TB/s HBM. Tip: set mode → LoRA and sweep W — the bar length does not move.
