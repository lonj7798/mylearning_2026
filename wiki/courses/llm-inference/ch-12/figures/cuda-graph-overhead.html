Caption. Launch overhead dominates decode latency at small batch sizes. CUDA Graphs eliminate it via static replay: the kernel sequence is captured once and re-issued with a single launch. The static shape constraint forces frameworks to capture one graph per discrete batch-size bucket (powers of two are typical), which is why vLLM / TensorRT-LLM precompile a small set of buckets at startup.
