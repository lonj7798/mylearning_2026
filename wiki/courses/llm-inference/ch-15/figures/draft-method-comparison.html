Draft Method Comparison

Five major speculative-decoding draft strategies, side by side.

Trade space. Each method moves on a different axis: training cost vs draft quality vs implementation simplicity. PLD is free for retrieval/copy workloads; EAGLE wins per-token acceptance; Medusa wins simplicity (drop-in heads); MTP wins because the model itself was pretrained to predict ahead. Modern serving stacks ship multiple to let the workload pick the best.
