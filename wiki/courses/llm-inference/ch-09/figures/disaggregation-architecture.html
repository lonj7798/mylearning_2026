Caption. Disaggregation separates the compute-bound prefill stage from the bandwidth-bound decode stage. KV transfer cost is the new bottleneck — solved with NVLink or RDMA. The trade is clean: fewer prefill workers can saturate compute, more decode workers can saturate HBM bandwidth, and each pool's batching policy is tuned to its own dominant cost.
