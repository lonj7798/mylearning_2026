Why this architecture matters. vLLM's separation of Scheduler (decides which sequences run this step) from BlockManager (owns the KV-cache page pool) is the canonical OSS LLM-serving design. Continuous batching, PagedAttention, chunked prefill, and automatic prefix caching (APC) are all realized as policies inside this Scheduler↔BlockManager interaction. Every modern serving engine (TGI, TensorRT-LLM, SGLang) follows variants of this split.
