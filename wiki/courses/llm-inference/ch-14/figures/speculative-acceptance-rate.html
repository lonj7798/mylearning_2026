What you should notice. E[accepted] = (1 - αK+1) / (1 - α) under iid matching. Doubling K barely helps when α is low — rejection happens early. The cost ratio sets the ceiling: even with perfect α, speedup ≤ K / (1 + 1/c). Production stacks pick K = 4–8 with a 10–20× cost ratio, giving typical 1.5–3× wall-clock speedups.
