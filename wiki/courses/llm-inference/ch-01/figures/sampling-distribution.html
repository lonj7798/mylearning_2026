Caption. Temperature, top-k, and top-p reshape the logit distribution. Lower temperature → more peaked → less diverse. Top-p truncates the long tail without fixed-k pathology: when the distribution is already peaked, top-p keeps just 1-2 tokens; when it's flat, top-p keeps many. Top-k always keeps exactly k regardless of context.
