How the model works

Disagreement rate ≈ (1−κ)/2 under balanced marginals; triple annotation with majority vote escalates only if all three disagree ≈ ((1−κ)/2)². Reviewer absorbs agreed-on doubles cheaply; adjudicator resolves the rest at 3–10× the rate.

Moving κ from 0.5 → 0.7 on the default config cuts the adjudicator line by roughly half — the dominant ROI of rubric iteration.

Simplifications: assumes balanced label marginals, no annotator dropout, no rubric version drift. Real operations additionally pay for onboarding, calibration sessions (~1 week of every annotator's time at project start), and anchor-set re-runs for drift detection.
