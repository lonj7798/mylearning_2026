Per-pair loss and gradient weight against the margin

Loss of a single preference pair as a function of its margin. In summed mode the margin is h = log[π_θ(y_w)/π_ref(y_w)] − log[π_θ(y_l)/π_ref(y_l)], the quantity DPO and IPO score (DPO Eq. 7; IPO Eq. 17). In per-token mode the margin is the difference of average token log-probabilities, the quantity SimPO scores (SimPO Eq. 6); the two margins are not in the same units, which is why their β values differ by an order of magnitude.

Move the pointer over the plot to read the loss and the gradient weight at that margin.

Parameters

Flip threshold

For a pair whose reference log-ratio is log[π_ref(y_l)/π_ref(y_w)] = c, the trained policy ranks that pair correctly if and only if its DPO loss is at most −log σ(βc) (Chen et al. 2024, Theorem 4.1). The red line marks that loss value; the pair must reach it before the ranking flips.

5

Measured ranking accuracy of released DPO checkpoints on the AlpacaFarm validation split is 53–54% length-normalized and 40–42% unnormalized (Chen et al. 2024, Table 1).

Defaults: β = 0.1 for DPO, the value reported in DPO App. B; β = 2.5 and γ = 1.4 for SimPO, the Llama-3-Instruct setting in SimPO App. B Table 8. Curves show one pair; a training batch averages many of them.
