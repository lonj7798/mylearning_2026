Variants

Reading the shapes

DPO: -log σ(βm). Saturates toward 0 as m→+∞ and grows linearly as m→-∞. No finite optimum — pushes m unboundedly when labels are deterministic (IPO's motivating failure).

IPO: (m - 1/(2τ))². Minimum at the finite target 1/(2τ). Larger τ → smaller target → weaker preference enforcement and closer to π_ref.

KTO: the desirable branch scores σ(β(r̂-z₀)) — loss decreases as the reward moves above the batch reference z₀. The undesirable branch is a mirror; together they form the prospect-theory concave-gain / steeper-loss asymmetry.

SimPO: -log σ(m - γ). The whole DPO curve shifts right by γ — the margin must exceed γ before the loss saturates. Per-token reward units mean β sits at ~2–10 here (not 0.1).
