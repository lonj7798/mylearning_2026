# Excerpt: K2-V2 — a 70B dense model whose released chain stops at SFT

Authors: K2 Team, Institute of Foundation Models, Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence
(correspondence hector.liu, eric.xing). Source type: official technical report. arXiv v2 dated 2026-01-26; the report
header is dated 2025-12-02.

## Released artifacts (front-matter table)
Model weights (`collections/LLM360/k2-v2`), training log (`llm360/K2-V2`), evaluation (`llm360/eval360`), training code
(`llm360/k2v2_train`), pre-training data (`datasets/LLM360/TxT360`), mid-training data (`datasets/LLM360/TxT360-Midas`),
SFT data (`datasets/LLM360/TxT360-3efforts`). The report calls this "360-open" and describes it as "a documentation of
the entire development-cycle, and a full suite of artifacts along the way" (§1).

## Stage chain
pre-training (12.25T tokens, 8,192 context) → four mid-training stages (2.719T tokens, context to 524,288) → SFT (3
epochs on 17.4B tokens per epoch). No preference-optimization stage and no RL stage are reported; §7 states that the
pass@k analysis makes the model "a promising candidate for further training with RL".

## Architecture and pre-training recipe (§3.1–§3.2)
- 80 layers, hidden 8,192, FFN 28,672, 64 attention heads of dimension 128, GQA with 8 key-value heads, RoPE θ = 500,000,
  RMSNorm ε = 1e-5, 70B dense parameters.
- Hyperparameter method: optimizer dynamics are characterized by "the effective averaging timescale τ_epoch", printed as
  a fraction of ηλD and B, which "approximates the effective number of epochs over which AdamW exponentially averages
  parameter updates". The target is tuned at 20 tokens per parameter and scaled proportionally to 1/√TPP (tokens per
  parameter) for the full run, following Wang and Aitchison (2025) and Bergsma et al. (2025a). A layout-preserving extraction renders the
  fraction ambiguously; two checks identify it as τ_epoch = B/(ηλD). First, the printed final values:
  9.8e6 / (1.5e-4 × 0.05 × 1.225e13) = 0.1067 against the printed target 0.1066, while ηλD/B would be 9.375. Second, a
  non-layout extraction of the same PDF emits `B` before `ηλD` (scratchpad `k2raw.txt` L506–507). Batch size is set by a scaling law for the batch that minimizes final
  validation loss at a fixed token budget, then "adjusted to align with MLSys constraints (accelerator counts)"; the
  learning rate is swept at small scale and extrapolated with the 1/d_model convention.
- Scheduler ablation: a 257M pilot trained on 41.48 × 10⁹ tokens at peak LR 1.88e-3, batch 0.9 × 10⁶ tokens and weight
  decay 0.137 gave SlimPajama validation loss 2.815 (cosine decay-to-zero), 2.845 (cosine decay-to-10%) and 2.817 (linear
  decay-to-zero). "These pilot results suggests that the specific shape of the decay is less critical than the peak and
  final values."
- Final run: η = 1.5e-4, "selected by referring to similar scale runs such as Llama3-70B ... and validating against our
  small-scale extrapolations"; the scaling law suggested 1,136 sequences of 8,192 tokens and the run used 1,200, giving
  B = 9.8 × 10⁶ tokens; D = 12.25 × 10¹² tokens; T = 1.25 × 10⁶ steps; weight decay λ = 0.05 for a target
  τ_epoch = 0.1066.
- Schedule change during the run: "during the main run we observed that the training loss and parameter norm became stale
  when the learning rate dropped below 1% of the peak. We therefore modified the schedule to decay to a lower bound of
  1.5 × 10⁻⁶ and held it constant for the remainder of pre-training."
- Hardware: an H200 cluster, eight GPUs per server at 700 W and 141 GB, eight 400 Gb/s InfiniBand HCAs per node (§3.4.1).
  No GPU-hour total is printed.

## Mid-training (§4.1, Table 3)
| Stage | Context | Tokens | RoPE base | Max LR | Min LR | LR decay | Context parallel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mid-1 | 8,192 | 1769B | 0.5M | 1.5e-5 | 6e-6 | cosine | 1 |
| mid-2 | 65,536 | 590B | 1M | 6e-6 | 6e-6 | constant | 1 |
| mid-3 | 131,072 | 229B | 10M | 6e-6 | 6e-6 | constant | 2 |
| mid-4 | 524,288 | 131B | 10M | 6e-6 | 6e-6 | constant | 8 |

- mid-1 "aims to shift from pre-training toward higher-quality data while beginning to introduce reasoning behaviors and
  thinking traces", with a global batch of 13,107,200 tokens. For mid-2 to mid-4, "we keep a constant learning rate of
  6 × 10⁻⁶" while extending context, and the report states that raising the RoPE base "significantly improves
  long-context" behaviour.
- Stage-4 cost: "We observe mild degradation in stage 4 where we focused on long-context data above 128k as it is hard to
  obtain high-quality reasoning data of such length and we instead primarily relied on code and books" (§5).

## SFT (§6.1–§6.3)
- Data: questions from permissively licensed or synthetic sources, "quality-filtered, deduplicated using substring
  matching, and decontaminated against evaluation benchmarks. The answers are mostly regenerated using GPT-OSS-120B at
  low, medium and high reasoning effort levels."
- Three reasoning efforts are induced by the special tokens `<think>`, `<think_fast>`, `<think_faster>`; tool calls follow
  the Model Context Protocol format with `<tools>` and `<tool_calls>` tokens.
- Training: 3 epochs, about 6,300 steps, 17.4B tokens per epoch, global batch 128, micro-batch 1, sequence length 65,536,
  RoPE base 10M "consistent with the final mid-training checkpoint", β1 0.9, β2 0.95, ε 1e-8, weight decay 0.05, cosine
  schedule with 10% warmup peaking at 2e-5 and decaying to 10% of the peak. Full-parameter SFT on the final mid-training
  checkpoint, with online best-fit packing: average truncation ratio 0, average padding ratio below 0.004%, against "more
  than 95%" padding under naive batching (Fig. 21).

## Memorization measurement (Table 4, §5)
Sentence-level memorization rate (%) measured by beam search (width 10, up to 20 tokens at a time) conditioned on each
sentence prefix of AIME questions: K2 23.32 (AIME 2024) and 14.43 (AIME 2025); Qwen2.5 72B 41.96 and 23.37; Llama 3.1 70B
9.84 and 9.85; Olmo 3 32B 37.82 and 14.47. The report's motivation: "prior works reported that effect of such
decontamination on benchmark performance is unclear".
