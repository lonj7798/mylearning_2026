Illustrative pool, not source data: two equal domains of 2,000 documents each. Ratings in domain A are normal with mean +m, in domain B with mean −m, both with standard deviation 0.71 (at m = 0.71 the pool's rating variance is about 1, as in QuRating's normalization). Selection keeps a share f of documents. Top-k keeps the highest ratings. Sampling uses p(d) ∝ exp(s/τ) without replacement; the figure computes the large-pool limit of Gumbel top-k sampling, where a document with rating s is kept with probability 1 − exp(−exp(s/τ − c)) and c is set so the expected kept share equals f. Defaults reproduce the ch-10a §5.1 numbers: domain B share 5.6% (top-k), 25.1% (τ = 1), 35.9% (τ = 2), 50% (uniform).
