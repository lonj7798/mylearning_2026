Learning-rate schedules and batch size

Companion to ch-03 §1 and §3. Panel A compares schedule shapes and shows the learning rate at a chosen stop point. Panel B computes the steps–examples trade-off and the best step size for a batch size, given a gradient noise scale.

A. Schedule shapes and the learning rate at a stop point

x axis: fraction of the planned run. y axis: learning rate divided by the run's peak (or constant) learning rate.

Scheduleη / η_peak at stop pointDecay finished at stop point?

Cosine with horizon = planned run: η_min + ½(η_peak − η_min)(1 + cos(π·progress)). WSD branch: constant after warmup, then a decay over the chosen fraction ending at the stop point; linear and (1-sqrt) shapes from Hägele et al. 2024 (arXiv:2405.18392 §3.2). MiniCPM reports that a decay over 10% of tokens was sufficient and 2.5% fell short (arXiv:2404.06395 §4.3). Published presets, each on its own run length: DeepSeek LLM multi-step, 31.6% of peak after 80% of tokens and 10% after 90% (arXiv:2401.02954 §2.3); Llama 3.1 405B cosine to 1% of peak over 1,200,000 steps with 8,000 warmup steps (arXiv:2407.21783 §3.4.1; the plot assumes warmup is inside the 1,200,000 steps and omits the final 40M-token anneal, which is not visible at this scale); OLMo 2 7B as released: cosine planned to 10% over 5T tokens, stopped at step 928,646 (3.895T tokens) where the stage-2 config starts at 6.1499e-5 = 0.205 of the 3e-4 peak, then linear to 0 over 50B tokens (arXiv:2501.00656 Table 3; configs/official-1124 OLMo2-7B-stage1.yaml and stage2-seed42.yaml). Warmup is drawn at 1% of the run for the generic curves; its true length differs by run.

B. Batch size, steps, examples, and the best step size

BSteps / S_min = 1 + B_noise/BExamples / E_min = 1 + B/B_noiseε_opt / ε_max = 1 / (1 + B_noise/B)ε_opt ratio when B doubles

Formulas from McCandlish et al. 2018 (arXiv:1812.06162 Eq. 2.6–2.12, App. D.1): S/S_min − 1 = (E/E_min − 1)^−1 and ε_opt(B) = ε_max/(1 + B_noise/B). At B = B_noise a run uses 2× the minimum steps and 2× the minimum examples. The table rows are B_noise/4, B_noise/2, B_noise, 2·B_noise, 4·B_noise and the current B; with B_noise = 1,000 they reproduce the ch-03 §3.1 table (5.0/1.25/0.20 at B = 250; 1.25/5.0/0.80 at B = 4,000). The model assumes SGD-like step-size behaviour under a quadratic loss; for Adam the same paper reports the best learning rate scaling as B^α with α between 0.5 and 1.0 before levelling off (§3.1, App. E.2). Measured critical batch sizes in LM pre-training grow mainly with tokens trained (Zhang et al. 2024, arXiv:2410.21676 §3.3).
