The leave-one-out advantage is A_i = r_i − (1/(N−1)) Σ_{j≠i} r_j, derived in the DeepSWE source card from the blog's §2.3 description; with N = 8 and 2 successes it gives +0.86 for a success and −0.29 for a failure. The GRPO option divides r_i − mean(r) by the reward standard deviation of the group; LOOP measures 61.9 against 71.3 AppWorld Test-Normal task goal completion for that division (arXiv:2502.01600v3, Table 1). Observation masking is Search-R1 §3.1 Eq. 2; its ablation is 0.431 against 0.343 average exact match over seven QA datasets (arXiv:2503.09516, Table 4). Loss normalization by the unmasked-token count is SimpleTIR Eq. 1 (arXiv:2509.02479v2). Masking harness-limited rollouts while keeping their reward is DeepSWE §2.3 and SkyRL-Agent §4.2 (arXiv:2511.16108v1). Token counts in the sliders are illustrative, not from any source.
