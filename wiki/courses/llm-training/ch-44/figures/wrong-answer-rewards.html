Wrong-answer rewards, abstention, and group advantages

Companion to ch-44 read.md, section "Negative samples and negative feedback".

A. One group of rollouts under a chosen reward function

OutcomeCountRewardAdvantage

Advantages follow the group-relative form used by GRPO, A_i = (r_i − mean(r)) / std(r) (arXiv:2402.03300v3), with the mean-only variant of Dr. GRPO and DeepSeek-V3.2 as the second option (arXiv:2512.02556 §3.1). Masked rollouts are excluded from both the statistics and the loss, which is DeepSWE's compact filtering (together.ai/blog/deepswe §2.3). The −10 option is Tülu 3's penalty for a response that does not end with an EOS token (arXiv:2411.15124v5 Table 21). Reward magnitudes are reader-set; Tülu 3 uses 10 for a correct answer and 0 otherwise (§6, Eq. 8).

B. The abstention threshold implied by the wrong-answer penalty

With reward +1 for a correct answer, −w for a wrong answer, and 0 for an abstention, answering has expected reward p − w(1 − p), which is positive exactly when p > w / (1 + w). The equivalent instruction in arXiv:2509.04664v1 §4.2 states a confidence target t with penalty t/(1 − t); the values printed there are t = 0.5 with penalty 1, t = 0.75 with penalty 2 (the formula gives 3), and t = 0.9 with penalty 9. That paper's claim is about evaluation scoring; the translation to an RL reward is the course's extension.
