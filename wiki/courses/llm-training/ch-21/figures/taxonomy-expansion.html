GLAN question sampling, corpus arithmetic, and fresh-test gaps

Companion to ch-21 §2 and §7. Panel A counts the key-concept combinations GLAN can sample from one syllabus. Panel B turns GLAN's reported ranges into derived corpus sizes. Panel C recomputes the GSM8k-versus-GSM1k gap test for models in the GSM1k appendix, or for accuracies you enter.

Panel A — Key-concept combinations for one question (GLAN, arXiv:2402.13064 §2.4)

A question is written from one or two class sessions and one to five key concepts. One session with m key concepts gives Σi=1..5 C(m, i) combinations. Two sessions with m1 and m2 key concepts give Σi=2..5 C(m1+m2, i) − Σi=2..5 C(m1, i) − Σi=2..5 C(m2, i): combinations of 2 to 5 concepts that use at least one concept from each session. GLAN reports "around five key concepts" per session (§3.1). The paper does not report how many combinations were actually sampled per syllabus.

Panel B — Derived corpus arithmetic (GLAN §3.1)

Reported: 126 disciplines; 100 to 200 subjects per discipline on average (10 GPT-4 queries per discipline); one syllabus per subject with 10 to 30 class sessions; around five key concepts per session; 10 million question-answer pairs in total. Every other number in this panel is derived from the slider values and is not reported by the paper.

Panel C — GSM8k minus GSM1k gap as a two-proportion z-test (arXiv:2405.00332 App. F)

z = (p8k − p1k) / sqrt( p̄(1 − p̄)(1/n8k + 1/n1k) ), with p̄ = (p8k·n8k + p1k·n1k)/(n8k + n1k). n1k = 1,205 GSM1k problems (§3); n8k = 1,319 GSM8k test problems (count from the MetaMath card, §4.1). Accuracies in the appendix are rounded to three decimals, so the recomputed z differs from the printed one in the second decimal. Rows are the standard-prompt results.

A significant gap shows that the GSM8k score overstates accuracy on problems written after the model's data was collected. It does not by itself show contamination: the GSM1k authors report only a 0.36 Spearman correlation between the gap and the model's likelihood of the GSM8k test set (§5.4).
