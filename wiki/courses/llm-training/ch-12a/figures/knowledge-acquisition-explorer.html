Repeat counts where a repeated subset degrades the model

Models trained for 100B tokens with part of the tokens drawn from repeats of a small subset. The shaded band marks repeat counts with at least 50% of the maximum degradation.

Left boundary E = 4.2e6 · N^−0.56 Right boundary E = 5.1e7 · N^−0.50

Source: Hernandez et al., arXiv:2205.10487v1, §2 and Figure 4 (boundary fits aggregated over 3-90% repeated data at 100B training tokens). The subset size in the readout is derived: share × 100B tokens ÷ repeat count. Values beyond the model sizes trained in the paper are an extrapolation of the fit, which the authors present as a prediction, not a measurement.
