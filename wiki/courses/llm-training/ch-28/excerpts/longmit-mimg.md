# Excerpt: LongMIT — multi-hop long-context instruction synthesis with a verification agent

**Paper:** Zhi Chen, Qiguang Chen, Libo Qin, Qipeng Guo, Haijun Lv, Yicheng Zou, et al. (Shanghai AI Laboratory; Harbin Institute of Technology; Central South University). arXiv 2409.01893, v1 2024-09, read at v2 (2025-05-19). Code and data: github.com/WowCZ/LongMIT.

**Why this excerpt exists:** the library card `longmit` describes a generic "class" of multi-turn long-conversation datasets with no authors or URL. The artifact named LongMIT is this paper. It is multi-hop question data, not multi-turn dialogue.

## Problem measured (Abstract; §1)

> "our preliminary experiments show that fewer than 35% of samples generated by Qwen2-72B are multi-hop, and over 40% exhibit poor quality" (Abstract)

§1 states the same pilot with manual annotation: under 35% multi-hop, 60% high quality, and "over 45% of the samples exhibiting semantic duplication".

## MIMG framework (§2, Fig. 2)

Four components, in order:
1. **Quality Verification Agent (QVA):** an LLM scores each sample; samples with score above a threshold θ are kept (Eq. 1). Criteria: relevance to the document, clarity, factual accuracy, logical coherence, complexity (§2.1). In the released pipeline the verifier is InternLM2-20B with threshold 8.5, outputting `{"in_document": BOOL, "domain_similarity": NUMBER, "quality": NUMBER}` after a rationale (App. C.4.2).
2. **Single-hop Question Generation Agent (SQGA):** generates single-hop questions per document; question-then-answer order is compared with unified generation (§2.2).
3. **Multiple Question Sampling (MQS):** builds a question-semantic relevance matrix with embeddings, BM25 or LDA, then samples related questions within one document (intra-document) or across documents (inter-document) (§2.3).
4. **Multi-hop Question Merging Agent (MQMA):** merges sampled single-hop questions into one multi-hop question (Eq. 6) (§2.4).

## Findings on synthesis choices (§3)

- Scoring beats binary classification as a verifier; LLM verifiers show low kappa agreement with human annotators on long inputs but high precision as selectors (§3.1.1, Fig. 3).
- More scoring criteria reduce long-context judgment bias; fewer than three criteria give limited improvement (§3.1.2, Fig. 4a).
- Adding a rationale to verification: without it, "performance drops by more than 8.6% across different domains" (§3.1.2, Fig. 4c).
- Question-then-answer generation improves retention and quality, most for open models (§3.2.2, Fig. 5b). Rationale during single-hop generation gives minimal improvement while token use triples (§3.2.2, Fig. 5c).
- BGE embedding similarity selects more relevant questions than BM25 or LDA; question-based sampling beats document-based sampling (§3.3.1, Fig. 6).
- Intra-document sampling gives higher quality and lower diversity; inter-document sampling the reverse (§3.3.2).
- For merging, rationales "fail to contribute" and "multi-hop synthesis should avoid using rationales" (§3.4).

Human-labelled quality (App. D.4, Table 7), Qwen-72B-Instruct as backbone: Self-Instruct 61.3 high-quality / 53.4 diversity / 33.1 multi-hop; MIMG 94.8 / 88.2 / 94.8.

## Dataset and training (App. C.3, E.1; Table 3)

- Documents from pre-training datasets, 9 domains, 2 languages; generation by Qwen2-72B-Instruct, verification by InternLM2-20B (App. C.3).
- Size (Table 3): LongMIT 64.40k samples, 5.07B tokens; LongAlign 9.89k / 0.17B; LongAlpaca 12.00k / 0.11B; ChatQA2 128.00k / 1.22B; NQ 315.20k / 4.56B.
- Long samples are formed by refilling the context "with other documents to a fixed length" after generation (App. C.2).
- Training: 64 A800 80G, DeepSpeed ZeRO-1, max sequence length 4K to 128K, Adam LR 3×10^-5, β1 0.9, β2 0.95, packing, global batch 4M tokens, 1 epoch (App. E.1).

## Results (§4.2, Table 1; App. D.5, Tables 6 and 8)

LongBench QA subsets scored by GPT-4o, average (Table 1):

| Base model | +ChatQA2 | +LongAlign | +LongAlpaca | +NQ | +LongMIT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| InternLM2-1.8B | 38.56 | 43.06 | 41.69 | 37.06 | 50.60 |
| LLaMA3-8B | 46.81 | 52.92 | 52.02 | 52.56 | 64.29 |
| InternLM2-7B | 44.71 | 57.71 | 52.35 | 48.47 | 67.48 |

The datasets differ in size (Table 3), so the comparison is not token-matched. Short-context instruction following on a Llama-3-8B-ProLong base: IFEval inst-level loose accuracy 0.1259 → 0.1583 with LongMIT and 0.0156 with ChatQA2 (Table 6); ArenaHard 7.2 → 6.7 with LongMIT (95% CI about ±1.1) and 4.4 with ChatQA2 (Table 8).
