What the slider does

Schematic 2-D projection of hidden states. Green cluster = retain set; red cluster = harmful trajectory from the paired (prompt, harmful completion) data; orange dashed line = decision surface.

Before RR: harmful cluster intact; defense lives on the input-side refusal classifier. Adversarial suffixes move the input across it while the harmful representation is untouched.

After RR: harmful cluster rerouted. Raising the retain weight recovers benign-set behavior but weakens rerouting; lowering it sharpens the defense but risks retain-set drift and over-refusal. Attested mechanism from [[circuit-breakers-data]].

Refusal-rate bands are rough anchors from raw-data reports: Llama-2-Chat-70B ≈ 95% clean / 75% attacked on Salad-Bench; GPT-4 ≈ 97% / 85%; best 2024 open instruct models in the mid-70s under attack. HarmBench contextual/multimodal leaves and Salad-Bench's 66 leaves run lower sample counts per leaf, so per-leaf numbers are noisier than aggregate.
