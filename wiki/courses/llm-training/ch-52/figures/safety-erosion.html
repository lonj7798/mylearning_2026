Two measured curves from ch-52 §5, on the same model (Llama-2-7B-Chat) and the same benchmark (HEx-PHI, 330 harmful instructions). Move each slider to read the value at that point. Both curves rise steeply in the first few units, which is the argument for re-running a safety gate on the shipped checkpoint rather than inheriting a number.

Llama-2-7B-Chat fine-tuned on 100 (harmful instruction, harmful answer) pairs, learning rate 2e-5, batch size 64. Values: 1.5% at 0 steps, 22.4% at 2, 76.4% at 4, 87.9% at 6 (Shallow Safety Alignment, arXiv:2406.05946v1, Fig. 3). At batch 64, six steps is 384 examples.
