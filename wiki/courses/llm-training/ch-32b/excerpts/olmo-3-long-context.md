# Excerpt: Olmo 3 — long-context extension (Stage 3)

- **Authors:** Team Olmo (Allen Institute for AI)
- **Year:** 2025 (arXiv v1 2025-12; v2 2026-04, used here)
- **Source type:** official technical report; released training scripts
- **Used in:** ch-32b §3, §4, §5, Recipe

## Recipe summary (§3.6)
- "We mix 34% long-context data with 66% high-quality short-context data sampled from Dolma 3 Dolmino Mix, and
  train using this mix for an additional 50B tokens for Olmo 3 7B and 100B tokens for Olmo 3 32B."
- "we apply YaRN (Peng et al., 2023) to full attention layers, and do not adjust positional embeddings on
  sliding-window attention layers; we use document packing and inter-document masking."
- Architecture (§3.2): sliding-window attention with window 4096 "at three out of every four layers", last layer
  always full attention.
- RULER is the development suite; HELMET is held out as an unseen suite (§3.6, footnote 25 notes partial overlap).
- Long data: olmOCR science PDFs, gzip-compressibility filter removing the 20% most and 20% least compressible
  text (§3.6.1); synthetic CWE and REX aggregation tasks generated by OLMo 2 Instruct 32B (§3.6.2).

## Survey of other recipes, as stated by the Olmo 3 authors (§3.6 introduction)
- Extension budgets "ranges from hundreds of billions (SmolLM3: 100B ...; GLM 4.5: 100B ...; DeepSeek V3: 123B ...;
  Apertus: 225B ...) to almost one trillion tokens (Kimi K2: 400B ...; Llama 3.1: 800B ...; DeepSeek V3.1: 840B
  ...)"; "AFM ... and Nemotron Nano 2 ... both use fewer than 20 billion tokens to extend to 64K and 128K".
- Placement: "Llama 3.1 models apply long-context extension prior to midtraining, Qwen 2.5 and 3 perform it
  afterwards, and GLM 4.5 applies extension only after supervised finetuning."
These are secondary reports; the chapter uses them only as the Olmo 3 authors' summary.

## Mixture ablation (§3.6.3)
"Early experiments on a 10B-token extension show that a 66% / 34% mix of long-context to short-context data drops
performance on a subset of OlmoBaseEval by 2.5 points; in comparison, a 34% long-context, 66% short-context mix
only drops performance by 0.8 points."

## RoPE extension choice (§3.6.4, Fig. 13a)
Adjusted base frequency, PI, and YaRN were each applied "either to all RoPE instances or ... restricted to RoPE
used in full attention layers. We find that applying YaRN only to full attention layers yields the best overall
performance." Other components: best-fit document packing, intra-document masking, 8-way context parallelism,
and for 32B a merge of the last three checkpoints (steps 10,000, 11,000, 11,921).

`with_rope_scaling(self, rope_scaling, full_attn_layers_only: bool = True)` applies scaling only to full-attention
layers by default.
