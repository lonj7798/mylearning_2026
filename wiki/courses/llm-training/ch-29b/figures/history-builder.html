Evidence-injection history builder

Build one synthetic chat history of a target length by inserting evidence sessions into filler sessions, in the way LongMemEval constructs its histories. The panel reports session counts, the filler mix, where the evidence falls by decile, and how many 5,000-token chunks a MemAgent-style reader would process.

Evidence by decile of the history
Defaults follow LongMemEval_S: about 115K tokens and about 50 sessions per question (so about 2.3K tokens per session, derived), and a filler pool of 25% ShareGPT, 25% UltraChat, and 50% simulated sessions from other non-conflicting attributes (longmemeval, §3.2, §3.4, App. A.2). The decile check follows BEAM App. B.5 Table 7, which reports that at 10M tokens 0.00% of probing questions draw evidence from the first decile (beam-10m-conversations). The chunk count uses MemAgent's 5,000-token chunk and 1,024-token memory (memagent, §3.1). Session lengths here are uniform; real sessions vary, so counts are approximate. The filler mix is rounded to whole sessions. The evidence-session control runs from 1 to 6; that range is a control of this figure, not a value from LongMemEval.
