Published-literature deltas this grid draws from

Dolma Table 5 ablation (Soldaini et al. 2024 §6): URL-dedup +0.4 avg, lang-ID +1.1, Gopher-style quality +1.9, content-filter neutral, paragraph-dedup +0.8. Applied here as the lang-ID (+lang) and perplexity (+ppl) rows since our small-scale run has no URL list and collapses content-filter into quality.

FineWeb Figure 4 (Penedo et al. 2024 §3): exact + near-dedup together deliver +2.4 HellaSwag but a known LAMBADA regression because dedup removes documents that locally match LAMBADA's continuation style. The memo should reproduce this sign pattern on its slice.

FineWeb §3.3: per-snapshot MinHash beats global MinHash by ~+0.6 on MMLU — the single most novel ablation in the paper; stretch-goal row in the lab.

Lee et al. 2021 §5: train/val overlap of 3.2% in C4 alone; decontamination FP rate at n=13 is 5–15%, at n=8 is ~50%. Not plotted here — it is a scalar the memo must report.
