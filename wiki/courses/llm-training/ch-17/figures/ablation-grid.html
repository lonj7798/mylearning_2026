Epoch rule: up to about 4 epochs of repeated data changes held-out loss negligibly, and downstream scores start dropping after about 4 epochs (Muennighoff et al., arXiv:2305.16264v5, Abstract, §6, App. L). A cell above 4 epochs is reported as confounded, because it measures repetition as well as the filter.
