# Excerpt: Tülu 3 — SFT mixture, decontamination rule, seed variance, and the unseen evaluation split

**Artifact:** Lambert, Morrison, Pyatkin, Huang, Ivison, Brahman, et al., "Tülu 3: Pushing Frontiers in Open Language Model Post-Training" (arXiv v1 2024-11; Allen Institute for AI). Official technical report. Loci are section and table numbers of the arXiv PDF.

## Evaluation suite: development and unseen (§2.2, Table 3)

| Core skill | Development | Unseen |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | MMLU, PopQA, TruthfulQA | MMLU-Pro, GPQA |
| Reasoning | BigBenchHard, DROP | AGIEval English |
| Math | MATH, GSM8K | Deepmind Mathematics |
| Coding | HumanEval (pass@10), HumanEval+ | BigcodeBench (pass@10) |
| Instruction following | IFEval, AlpacaEval 2 (win rate) | IFEval-OOD, HREF |
| Safety | Tülu 3 Safety (average) | — |

- "We did not examine scores on our unseen set when developing our models" (§2.2). There is no unseen safety evaluation (§2.2).
- Unseen task formulations were designed separately and follow realistic usage: no few-shot examples presented as dialog, clear instructions with an answer format, lenient answer extraction (§7.3).

## Prompt decontamination (§3.2)

- Full-string, n-gram, and embedding matching were tried. n-gram matching was the most useful; with embeddings "it difficult to distinguish mere distributional similarity from actual paraphrasing" (§3.2).
- Overlap is computed on prompts only (user turns), because completions are often regenerated by language models (§3.2).
- Rule: a test-instance token matches a training instance if the two share an 8-gram containing that token; the test instance overlaps that training instance if more than 50% of its tokens match (§3.2).
- A training set is contaminated if its instances overlap with more than 2% of the instances of any development or unseen evaluation. Sets contaminated with unseen evaluations were removed entirely; for development evaluations, the whole set was removed when that did not significantly hurt performance, otherwise the matching instances were removed (§3.2).
- Examples of removed shares (Table 8): Evol CodeAlpaca 3.5% (HumanEval); WildChat GPT-4 5.4% (safety); NuminaMath-TIR 11.3% (MATH).

## SFT mixture and ablations (§4.1–4.2)

- Final SFT mixture: 939,344 prompts (Table 7, SFT column total).
- Tülu 3 Persona IF: 29,980 verifiable instruction-response pairs generated with GPT-4o from 33 hand-written seed instructions that cover the 25 constraint types defined in IFEval (§3.1.2). IFEval is therefore a targeted benchmark for this mixture, not a held-out one.
- Mixture design: skill-specific mixtures first, keeping the mixture best on each skill; then combination, iteration, decontamination, and downsampling of large sets (§4.1.2).
- Table 10 (Tülu 3 8B SFT, full vs one data group removed):

| Arm | Avg | GSM8K | MATH | IFEval | AlpacaEval 2 | Safety |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tülu 3 8B SFT | 60.1 | 76.2 | 31.5 | 72.8 | 12.4 | 93.1 |
| w/o WildChat | 58.9 | 75.8 | 31.8 | 70.1 | 7.5 | 95.2 |
| w/o Safety | 58.0 | 76.9 | 32.6 | 71.0 | 12.4 | 74.7 |
| w/o Persona data | 58.6 | 76.8 | 30.1 | 53.6 | 13.5 | 93.9 |
| w/o Math data | 58.2 | 64.1 | 23.5 | 70.6 | 12.0 | 93.5 |

- Stratified subsamples of the mixture: the average keeps improving as data is added; TruthfulQA drops as the amount of data increases (§4.2, Figure 4).

## SFT training settings (§4.3, Table 11)

| Setting | 8B | 70B |
|---|---|---|
| learning rate; schedule | 5 × 10⁻⁶; linear | 2 × 10⁻⁶; linear |
| effective batch; max tokens | 128; 4,096 | 128; 4,096 |
| warmup ratio; epochs | 0.03; 2 | 0.03; 2 |

- Loss: a sum over tokens instead of a mean, which "requires an adjustment to learning rates"; with the sum loss, 5e-6 worked best and training longer than 2 epochs gave no further improvement (§4.3, Figures 5–6).
- Compute: the final 8B SFT model trained on 32 GPUs for 6 hours (§4.3).

## Seed variance (§4.3.1, Table 14)

| Model | Seeds (average score) |
|---|---|
| Tülu 3 8B SFT | 42: 59.9; 123: 60.1; 456: 59.8; 789: 59.8; 1011: 59.8 |
| Tülu 3 70B SFT | 42: 71.8; 123: 70.0; 456: 72.6 |

- "SFT performance noticeably varies based on the seed, highlighting the importance of multiple training runs" (§4.3.1). The 70B spread is 2.6 points; the 8B spread is 0.3 points.

## Use in ch-29

- The general-mix baseline arm is a random subset of the Tülu 3 SFT mixture of the same size as the lab pool, decontaminated with the §3.2 rule against the lab's evaluation suite. Because Persona IF targets IFEval's constraint types, the baseline's IFEval column is read as a targeted score, and IFEval-OOD or IFBench is the held-out instruction-following check for both arms.
- The development/unseen split is the model for the lab's rule that filter thresholds are chosen on development benchmarks and the final comparison is read on benchmarks not used for those choices.
- Table 10 shows that removing one data group moves the skill it targets (GSM8K −12.1 without math data; IFEval −19.2 without Persona data). The lab's per-filter category report exists to detect the same effect when a filter removes a category.
