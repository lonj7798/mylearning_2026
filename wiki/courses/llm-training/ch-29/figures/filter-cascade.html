1. Survival counts (stage order: format → dedup → IFD → verifier)

Formula: N_k = N_raw · s_1 · … · s_k; verifier survival = 1 − (checkable share) × (1 − pass rate). The earlier version of this lab used 10,000 raw rows with survival rates 0.78 (format), 0.15 (IFD), 0.897 (dedup), and 0.943 (verifier) and ended at 990 rows, not at its 5,000-row target.

2. Category shares before and after IFD selection

Edit the counts and keep rates. The direction of the defaults follows Cherry LLM Table 4 (top 5% by IFD led by "write story" and "generate story", bottom 5% by "rewrite sentence" and "edit sentence"); the numbers themselves are illustrative. With the defaults the total keep share is 0.15, as planned, while the rewriting share falls from 20% to 4%.

3. MinHash-LSH candidate probability

P(candidate | Jaccard = s) = 1 − (1 − s^r)^b. Lee et al. (arXiv:2107.06499 §4.2, App. A) use 9,000 hashes in 450 buckets of 20 and keep pairs with Jaccard > 0.8 and edit similarity > 0.8. A pair must first become a candidate and then pass the confirmation threshold. A higher confirmation threshold marks fewer pairs as duplicates and removes fewer rows.
