Reward from SWE-RL Eq. 1: −1 if the response has a wrong format, otherwise difflib.SequenceMatcher(None, pred, oracle).ratio() = 2M / (len(pred) + len(oracle)). Advantages follow GRPO: Ai = (ri − mean) / std over the group. This page uses the population standard deviation; the paper does not state which estimator is used. The similarity is computed with the same longest-matching-block procedure as Python's difflib, including its rule that ignores characters appearing in more than 1% of the oracle when the oracle has 200 or more characters.
