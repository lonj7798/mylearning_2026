How the rules differ. RULER compares every model with one fixed score, 85.6, the 13-task average of Llama2-7B at 4K. NoLiMa compares each model with 85% of its own base score, where the base score is the best score among the 250, 500 and 1K lengths averaged over question-needle pairs (the base and the threshold printed in NoLiMa Table 3 are used here). Applying the relative rule to a RULER row uses the 4K score as the base; the RULER paper does not do this, and the result is shown only to compare the rules.

Sources. RULER: Hsieh et al., arXiv:2404.06654v3, Table 3 (threshold and weighted averages, §4). NoLiMa: Modarressi et al., arXiv:2502.05167v3, Table 3 (base score and 0.85 rule, §4.3). The weights 1-6 are a derivation that reproduces the printed wAvg values; see ch-32c read.md §1.
