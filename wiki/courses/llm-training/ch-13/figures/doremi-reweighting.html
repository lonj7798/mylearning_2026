Mixture Weight Workbench

Companion to ch-13 read.md. Panel A performs the domain-weight update of DoReMi Algorithm 1 on numbers you enter. Panel B converts a mixture into epochs per domain and checks a repetition cap. Panel C plots the published Pile domain weights from two DoReMi proxy sizes. Panels A and B use toy inputs; Panel C copies a source table.

A. One DoReMi weight update (Algorithm 1, step 2)

Enter per-token excess losses ℓθ − ℓref for two tokens of each domain in the minibatch. The defaults are the worked example in read.md §2. Paper settings: η = 1 and c = 1e-3 in all experiments (DoReMi, arXiv:2305.10429v4 §2).

Domaintoken 1token 2αt−1λtα′t = αt−1·eηλnormalizedαt after smoothing

Solid lines: αt when the same λ is applied at every step. Dashed lines: the running average ᾱ, which is what DoReMi returns. Holding λ fixed shows only the update rule. In a real run λ changes every step, because a larger weight scales up the proxy's updates on that domain and lowers its excess loss. Minibatches are sampled with uniform domain weights; α rescales the loss.
