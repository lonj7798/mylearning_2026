DoReMi — exponentiated-gradient reweighting across domains

A proxy LM's per-domain excess loss drives an adversary that reweights the mix. Adjust η (EG step) and watch α converge. Domain "difficulty" is simulated so you can see the equilibrium without a real LM.

Domain weights α over time

Starts uniform; EG update concentrates mass on domains with high excess loss. Time-averaged ᾱ is the transferable weight vector.

Per-domain excess loss ℓ_k

Proxy loss minus reference loss on each domain. The adversary reads this vector and reweights. Pareto result: all ℓ_k fall, even for downweighted domains.
