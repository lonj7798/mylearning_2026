Panel 1 — pass@k for seven training signals

Benchmark:

PSR trains only on correct rollouts, NSR only on incorrect ones, W-REINFORCE combines them with the positive term scaled by λ = 0.1. Pass@1 orders the methods one way and pass@256 orders them differently: on MATH, PSR reaches pass@1 74.1 with pass@256 91.2, while the base model it started from has pass@256 96.9.

Panel 2 — pass@1 and pass@256 across GRPO training steps

Set:

Left axis is shared. On Omni-MATH-Train, pass@1 rises 9.9 → 26.1 → 33.6 → 42.5 across base, step 150, step 300 and step 450, while pass@256 falls 67.2 → 66.3 → 65.3 → 64.3. A run stopped at the step with the best pass@1 is the run with the lowest coverage of the four.
