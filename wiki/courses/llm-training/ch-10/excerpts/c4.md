# Excerpt: C4 in "Exploring the Limits of Transfer Learning with a Unified Text-to-Text Transformer"

Raffel, Shazeer, Roberts, Lee, Narang, Matena, et al. (Google), arXiv 2019-10, JMLR 2020. Library card: [[c4]]. Loci refer to arXiv v4.

## Rules (§2.2)
Source: Common Crawl "web extracted text" from April 2019.
- Retain only lines ending in a terminal punctuation mark (period, exclamation mark, question mark, end quotation mark).
- Discard pages with fewer than 3 sentences; retain only lines with at least 5 words.
- Remove any page containing a word on the "List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene or Otherwise Bad Words".
- Remove lines containing "Javascript".
- Remove pages containing "lorem ipsum".
- Remove pages containing a curly bracket "{", because it "appears in many programming languages (such as Javascript, widely used on the web) but not in natural text".
- Remove citation markers such as [1] and [citation needed].
- Remove lines containing "terms of use", "privacy policy", "cookie policy", "uses cookies", "use of cookies", or "use cookies".
- "Discarded all but one of any three-sentence span occurring more than once in the data set."
- Keep pages classified as English by langdetect with probability at least 0.99.
Result: about 750 GB (745 GB in Table 8).

## Release code (separate artifact)
`tensorflow_datasets/text/c4_utils.py` (master, fetched 2026-09-14): `_MIN_WORDS_PER_LINE = 5`, `_MIN_NUM_SENTENCES = 3`, `_MAX_WORD_LENGTH = 1000`, `_END_MARKS = (".", "?", "!", '"')` (L41–44); lines ending in "..." are removed (L248); the page-removing `{` and "lorem ipsum" checks run after the end-mark and length checks (L248–266); `remove_duplicate_text` deduplicates hashed lines (L345–370), which differs from the three-sentence spans in §2.2.

## Data-set ablation (§3.4.1, Table 8)
T5 baseline (~220M), 2^35 tokens. C4 (745 GB) vs "C4, unfiltered" (6.1 TB, langdetect only): GLUE 83.28 vs 81.46; CNNDM 19.24 vs 19.14; SQuAD 80.88 vs 78.78; SuperGLUE 71.36 vs 68.04; EnDe 26.98 vs 26.55; EnFr 39.82 vs 39.34; EnRo 27.65 vs 27.21. Individual rules are not ablated.

## Audit of the released C4 (Dodge et al. 2021, arXiv:2104.08758v2; no library card; numbers as recorded in [[c4]])
- Table 1 (SpaCy tokens): C4.EN.NOCLEAN 1.1B documents / 1.4T tokens; C4.EN.NOBLOCKLIST 395M / 198B; C4.EN 365M / 156B.
- §5.1: of 100,000 blocklist-excluded documents in 50 clusters, 16 clusters (31% of excluded documents) are largely sexual; other clusters cover science, medicine, health, legal, and political text.
- §5.2: mentions of sexual orientations have the highest likelihood of removal; in 50 sampled documents mentioning "lesbian" and "gay", 22% and 36% are non-offensive or non-sexual.
- §5.3: AAE and Hispanic-aligned English documents are removed by the blocklist at 42% and 32%, White-aligned and other English at 6.2% and 7.2%; C4.EN is 97.8% White-aligned, 0.07% AAE, 0.09% Hispanic-aligned.
- §2 describes the thresholds as "fewer than three words" and "less than five sentences", which does not match T5 §2.2 and the TFDS code.
