3. Same document, three punctuation rules

Filled bars: lines ending in terminal punctuation. Other rules (minimum words per line, blocklists, repetition) are ignored, and each kept line is counted as one sentence.

C4: T5 §2.2 (line rule; pages with fewer than 3 sentences discarded). Dolma: App. N.4 datasheet wording of "C4 NoPunc" (§5.2 describes it as paragraph removal). FineWeb: §3.6 rule, remove when the fraction of lines ending in punctuation is ≤ 0.12.
