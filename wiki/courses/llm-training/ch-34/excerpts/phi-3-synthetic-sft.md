# Excerpt: Phi-3 — synthetic-heavy pretraining that blurs the SFT boundary

**Source library:** `wiki/raw-data/llm-training/model-reports/phi-3.md`
**Report:** Marah Abdin et al. (Microsoft), 2024, "Phi-3 Technical Report".

---

## Why this source anchors ch-34 §5

Phi-3 is the clearest 2024-era evidence that small-model post-training can be reframed as **pretraining data curation plus a refinement SFT**. The innovation is "entirely in the dataset" (source line 14): Phase 1 pretraining is heavily filtered web, Phase 2 is primarily synthetic textbook-like content. By the time the SFT stage begins, the model has already seen most of what other labs call SFT-shaped data.

---

## The two-phase pretraining curriculum

From the source (lines 31-36):

> ### Pretraining (context)
> - **Total tokens:** 3.3T (mini), 4.8T (small), 4.8T (medium).
> - **Two-phase curriculum:**
>   - **Phase 1:** majority of training tokens; heavily filtered web data.
>   - **Phase 2:** primarily synthetic tokens (textbook-like) + small allocation for ultra-filtered reasoning-heavy web data.
> - Synthetic data generated by larger LLMs (GPT-3.5 / GPT-4 class) covering math, coding, common-sense reasoning, world knowledge. Human reviewed.

The exact Phase 1 / Phase 2 token split is not publicly itemized for Phi-3. The raw-data source says "majority" for Phase 1 and "primarily synthetic" for Phase 2 — this is deliberately loose. The qualitative structure is what matters: Phase 2 is the *target-domain pretraining*, and its quality ceiling is set by the teacher models.

**Consequence for the SFT boundary.** When Phase 2 pretraining has already exposed the model to step-by-step math derivations, instruction-response pairs, and common-sense reasoning chains — all in a *textbook voice* — the explicit SFT stage is no longer the first time the model sees "how to answer instructions". SFT becomes a format-refinement + chat-template adaptation step rather than the primary capability acquisition stage. This is the Phi line's signature.

---

## The SFT stage — what's publicly disclosed

From the source (lines 38-41):

> ### SFT
> - **Purpose:** enhance instruction-following across diverse domains (math, coding, reasoning, conversation, model identity, safety).
> - **Data:** curated high-quality synthetic + human-written instruction-response pairs.
> - Specific size and mixture not itemized publicly for Phi-3.

The SFT data is **not itemized** — size, mix ratio, teacher model identity, filtering thresholds are all withheld. For ch-34's six-lab table, this means Phi-3's "SFT size" row is *nd*, and any comparison to Qwen 2.5's 1M or OLMo 2's 939K is across a different definition of the SFT stage.

## The safety-specific DPO slice

From the source (lines 43-49):

> ### DPO
> - **Purpose:** improve model safety and align with human preferences.
> - **Data:** chat-format data, reasoning-focused preferences, responsible-AI / safety preferences.
> - Specific beta, LR, batch size, and preference pair count not fully disclosed in the public report.
>
> ### Safety-specific DPO slice
> - Dedicated responsible AI preference data covering harmful content refusal, bias mitigation, privacy scenarios.
> - Evaluated via Microsoft's internal safety suite plus external benchmarks.

Phi-3's novel-at-the-time contribution on the preference side is the **dedicated safety slice**. The DPO pool is not a single undifferentiated preference set; it carries a named sub-pool for responsible AI — harmful content refusal, bias mitigation, privacy. This is the preference-pool analogue of what Tülu 3 does with per-capability SFT buckets (reasoning / chat / safety / IF): a named partition inside the DPO mix that lets Microsoft evaluate refusal-rate and bias separately from general chat quality.

## Benchmark framing

From the source (line 18):

> - Demonstration that 3.8B can reach ~Mixtral 8x7B quality on some benchmarks when pretraining data is synthetic-heavy.

This is the headline claim for Phi-3-mini. Not "matches Mixtral 8x7B across the board" — "on some benchmarks". The "some" qualifier is load-bearing: Phi-3 performs strongly on tasks in the distribution its synthetic pretraining targeted (MMLU, HellaSwag, math word problems) and more weakly outside that distribution (novel-domain reasoning, long-form generation).

The generalization ceiling is set by the teacher's coverage. If GPT-4 doesn't know a domain, Phi-3 won't either — and the synthetic generation cannot transcend the teacher's knowledge. This is the recurring critique of the Phi line that carries from [[phi-textbooks]] through Phi-4.

## The contamination concern

The Phi-3 source does not lead with contamination, but the Phi line's history makes it the elephant in the room. From [[phi-textbooks]]:

> later analyses flagged non-trivial overlap between synthetic exercises and HumanEval-style prompts — a perpetual critique of the Phi line

And from [[phi-1-5]]:

> **Topic taxonomy leakage:** the 20K topic list and benchmark coverage overlap is a perennial critique.

For ch-34's contamination-gate framing (ch-48), Phi-3 is the running example. When the pretraining data is synthesized by a teacher that has seen the eval benchmarks, every claim needs a contamination check. Microsoft does decontaminate, but the decontamination is against *exact* benchmark strings — not against paraphrased versions or stylistic mimics the teacher might produce.

## What Phi-3 does not disclose

- SFT data volume, mix composition, teacher model identity.
- DPO β, LR, batch size, preference-pair count.
- Phase 1 / Phase 2 token split (qualitative only).
- Teacher model versions (described as "GPT-3.5 / GPT-4 class" — unspecified which).
- Phase-2-pretraining synthetic token count absolute.
