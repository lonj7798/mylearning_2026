ch-38 — PPO clipped surrogate and KL-to-reward tradeoff

Companion to wiki/courses/llm-training/ch-38/read.md. Two interactive panels: Panel 1 scrubs the ratio r and advantage sign to expose why the pessimistic min flattens the gradient outside [1-ε, 1+ε]. Panel 2 sweeps β to trace the KL-vs-reward Pareto front annotated with reward-hacking and mode-collapse regimes.

Panel 1. L^CLIP(r, A) and its gradient

L^CPI = r·A (unclipped) clip(r, 1-ε, 1+ε)·A L^CLIP = min of the two dL^CLIP/dr (gradient)

Casework (A > 0): gradient lives inside [1-ε, 1+ε] and on the left tail where the unclipped term is pessimistic. Gradient is zero above 1+ε — the trust region. For A < 0, mirror.

Panel 2. β sweep — KL(π‖π_ref) vs reward

expected reward E[r] KL(π‖π_ref) objective E[r] - β·KL

Regimes: small β (left) → reward hacking, policy drifts outside RM calibration region. Large β (right) → mode collapse toward π_ref, RM signal ignored. InstructGPT picked β=0.02 on reward-scale-normalized RMs; Llama-2 picked β=0.01 to allow larger per-iteration drift under iterative RLHF.
