Mechanics. For softmax probabilities p = softmax(z), the cross-entropy loss −log py has gradient ∂L/∂zj = pj − 1[j = y]. Minimizing log pk for a negative token k has gradient 1[j = k] − pj, so every other logit rises by η·pj: the most likely token gains the most. The unlikelihood term −log(1 − pk) (Welleck et al., arXiv:1908.04319, Eq. 3; gradient in App. A, Eq. 12) has gradient pk on zk and −pk·pj/(1 − pk) on the others, so its size shrinks as pk → 0. The CRINGE term log(1 + exp(sk − s+)) (Adolphs et al., arXiv:2211.05826, Eq. 3–5) contrasts the negative logit with one positive token sampled from the model's top-k tokens other than k; this page applies the expected update over that top-k sample. Updates act on the four logits directly, not on network weights, so shared-parameter effects such as likelihood displacement across related responses are not shown (see read.md §2 and §5.6).
