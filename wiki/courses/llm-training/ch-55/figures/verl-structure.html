Every path, function name, default value and line range shown here was read at the pinned commit above and is recorded with the same locus in the chapter text and in the source cards verl-ppo-loss, verl-grpo, verl-rollout and entropy-logging-patterns. Two defaults are worth stating outside the cards, because published material often gives them wrongly: verl supports a KL term to the reference policy in the loss (actor.use_kl_loss) and in the reward (algorithm.use_kl_in_reward), and both default to off; and verl's documented Dr. GRPO aggregation mode is seq-mean-token-sum-norm, not seq-mean-token-sum.
