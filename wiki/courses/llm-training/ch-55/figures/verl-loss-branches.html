Which branch of verl's policy loss selects, and what the gradient is

Implements compute_policy_loss_vanilla at verl/trainer/ppo/core_algos.py L1336–1361, commit 753aed3. Move the controls to reproduce the two worked examples in §2 of the chapter by hand.

Defaults in verl: ε_low = ε_high = 0.2 (actor.yaml L39, L42), c = 3.0 (L83). The DAPO recipe sets ε_high = 0.28 (docs/algo/dapo.md L52–57).

Selected per-token loss against r at the current settings. Flat regions are the regions where the gradient is zero: the lower clip below 1−ε_low, the upper clip above 1+ε_high when A ≥ 0, and the dual-clip cap above c when A < 0.

Source of the numbers. The four expressions and the torch.where(advantages < 0, …) selection are quoted from core_algos.py L1336–1361 at commit 753aed3e1c286ba6825a74342b28669e72c083ea; the defaults are from verl/trainer/config/actor/actor.yaml L36–42 and L83. The gradient shown is −A·r on an unclipped branch and 0 on a clipped branch, which follows from the expressions above and is not printed by verl. The reason for the dual-clip bound is stated by Ye et al., arXiv:1912.09729v3 Eq. 5: with off-policy data and Ât < 0 a large ratio "will introduce a big and unbounded variance". This figure shows one token in isolation; the batch loss is the aggregation of these values under loss_agg_mode.
