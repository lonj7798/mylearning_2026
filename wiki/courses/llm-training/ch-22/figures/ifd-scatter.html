1. IFD: loss ratio (Cherry LLM) versus perplexity ratio (Superfiltering)

Samples(A|Q)s(A)loss ratiorank (loss)PPL ratiorank (PPL)

Loss ratio = s(A|Q) / s(A) (Cherry LLM Eq. 5). PPL ratio = exp(s(A|Q)) / exp(s(A)) = exp(s(A|Q) − s(A)) (Superfiltering Eq. 2). Cherry LLM removes samples with IFD > 1 and keeps the highest remaining values (rank 1 = kept first). The coronavirus and tweet rows are the worked examples in Cherry LLM App. F, Fig. 9; samples A and B are constructed to show that the two forms can order samples differently. Samples with IFD > 1 are marked "removed" and not ranked.
