12x12 pairwise cosine similarity of random-projected per-sample gradients from a frozen proxy LM. Raw pool has visible off-diagonal blocks (redundant modes). Prismatic-curated view resamples away from high-density clusters — off-diagonal mass flattens and G-Vendi rises. G-Vendi = exp(H_VN(rho)) with rho = K / tr(K).
How to read this pair. The left panel shows a *sample-level* score (IFD is per-sample). The right panel shows a *set-level* score (G-Vendi is a property of the whole kept set). Two different filters, two different geometries, two different failure modes. A pool that looks clean on IFD can still collapse in gradient space (high G-Vendi is not implied by a tight IFD distribution), and vice versa.
