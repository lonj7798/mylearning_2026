2. Paired audit: interval and minimum detectable effect

Model A is the headline benchmark, model B the audit counterpart, scored on the same items (binary scores). Enter how many items the two disagree on and what the mean difference is.

Formulas from Adding Error Bars to Evals §4.2 and §5: SE = sqrt(Var(s_A−B)/n), CI = s̄ ± 1.96·SE, δ = (z_.025 + z_β)·sqrt(ω²/n) with z_.025 = 1.96, z_.20 = 0.8416, one sample per item, so that ω² = Var(s_A−B). With binary paired scores and disagreement rate a+b, Var(s_A−B) = (a+b) − s̄². The unpaired standard error uses the two marginal rates and ignores the pairing.
