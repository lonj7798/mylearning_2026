Lee 2021 uses r=450, b=20 (m=9000). Under the formula above t ≈ 0.993, so LSH alone would accept nearly everything at J≥0.8. The paper runs an explicit Jaccard post-filter on every LSH candidate pair; the massive signature count is a recall-maxing choice, not a threshold-setting one. Typical production values are r=9, b=20 (m=180, t≈0.75) paired with a J≥0.8 verifier.

At τ=0.95 you remove near-copies; at τ=0.80 you remove topical redundants; at τ<0.70 you start deleting domain coverage. D4 observes that aggressive clustering (high k) plus low τ preferentially eats the long tail: MMLU creeps up while niche-domain evals fall. Use τ as a downstream-eval-gated knob, not a fixed duplicate threshold.
