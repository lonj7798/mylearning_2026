3. SCoRe's shaping bonus

SCoRe adds b = α·(r̂(y2) − r̂(y1)) to the second-attempt reward, and uses the instantaneous reward only (discount factor γ = 0), so the bonus enters the gradient of the second-attempt tokens (arXiv:2409.12917v2 §5.2, App. A). The paper uses α = 10 (App. B Table 5).

At α = 0 the two transitions that end correct carry the same second-attempt reward, so a policy that answers correctly at the first attempt and repeats itself is as good as one that repairs a wrong answer. Raising α separates them and makes correct→incorrect the most penalized transition. Measured effect on MATH500 (Table 4): Δ(t1,t2) 4.4% with shaping, 2.6% without.
