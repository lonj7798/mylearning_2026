Standard deviations use TRL's nanstd, which applies the unbiased correction count / (count − 1) (trl/trainer/utils.py L881-L885), and every division adds 1e-4 to the denominator as the code does. A sign disagreement means the default aggregation is pushing the policy in the opposite direction from the one the equal weights were meant to express — the failure described in read.md §4.3.
