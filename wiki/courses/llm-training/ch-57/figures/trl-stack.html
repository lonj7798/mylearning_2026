Decision gate summary

TRL wins when you stay on one node: datasets + tokenizer + Trainer + Accelerate is the fastest prototype-to-run path in open source. TRL loses the moment you need multi-node rollouts, async actor-rollout decoupling, or straggler-tolerant scheduling. At that point the bottleneck is always the Accelerate layer — swap to verl (Ray + split workers) or OpenRLHF (Ray + colocated vLLM).
