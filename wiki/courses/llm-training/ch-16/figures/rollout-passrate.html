    Under GRPO/RLOO the per-prompt gradient is proportional to reward variance across rollouts. For binary verifiers, variance is p(1−p) — peaks at p = 0.5, goes to 0 at p = 0 and p = 1. A prompt with p ≈ 0 or p ≈ 1 wastes rollouts. Source: [[replay-buffer-rlhf]], [[kimi-k1-5]], [[minibatch-sharing-rl]], [[reinforcement-learning-with-one-training-example]].
