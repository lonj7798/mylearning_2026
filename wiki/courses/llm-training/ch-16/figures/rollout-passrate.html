Exact values for G = 8, computed from the two formulas: P(zero-variance) is 0.0078 at p = 0.5, 0.100 at p = 0.75, 0.430 at p = 0.9 and 0.663 at p = 0.95. Single-group advantage mass 2k(G−k)/G is 4.00 at k = 4, 1.75 at k = 1 or 7, and 0 at k = 0 or 8. Two cut points are drawn as dashed lines: 0.75 — Llama-Nemotron discards prompts at or above this pass rate over 8 samples (arXiv:2505.00949v5 §5.1); 0.625 — Olmo 3 Think 7B discards prompts solved in more than 5 of 8 rollouts (arXiv:2512.13961v2 §4.4.2). The remaining published cut is at the two endpoints of the axis: DAPO keeps only groups with accuracy strictly between 0 and 1 (arXiv:2503.14476v2 §3.2), and Skywork-OR1 removes 0/N and N/N at N = 16 (arXiv:2505.22312v2 §6.2).

The arithmetic here is an illustration with user-chosen inputs, not a measured pool: no source publishes the per-domain pass-rate distribution of an RL pool. The effect it shows is reported: for Tülu 3 405B, GSM8K was removed from the RLVR mixture because SFT and DPO had saturated it and IFEval prompts were removed as unhelpful, leaving MATH as the only training domain (arXiv:2411.15124v5 §8.1). Olmo 3 downsampled eight OMEGA subtasks by 50% after offline filtering (arXiv:2512.13961v2 §4.4.2).
