The formula. Muennighoff 2023 fits D' = U · (1 − exp(−R / R_T)) with R_T ≈ 4. Marginal value of the k-th repetition is w(k) = exp(−(k−1) / R_T). By R = R_T you capture ~63% of the asymptote; by R = 3 · R_T you are at ~95% and fresh tokens are worth more than continued repetition if available.

Two bounds, one picture. Allen-Zhu Part 3.3 shows a transformer stores ~2N bits of factual knowledge at saturation — storage scales linearly with parameters, not with tokens. The recall curve P(k) = 1 − exp(−k/τ) shows that within that storage budget, rare facts need ~τ exposures before they become reliably extractable. A 7B model's 14 Gbit ceiling is reached regardless of training tokens; a fact seen once in 1T tokens is ≈0% recoverable at τ=300.
