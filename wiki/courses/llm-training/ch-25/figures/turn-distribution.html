Reported turn counts and message lengths in multi-turn corpora

Every bar is a number printed in a source (or a derived value marked as such). No distribution shapes are drawn, because the sources report averages, not histograms.

How to read this figure
  • "Turn" means different units in different papers. SODA reports 7.6 counting utterances (SODA Table 2); UltraChat re-measures SODA at 3.6 counting exchanges (UltraChat Table 5). Tick the conversion box to put utterance-based averages on the exchange scale.
  • LMSYS-Chat-1M and WildChat average 2.02 and 2.54 exchanges (ShareGPT 3.51, Chatbot Arena 1.2), and the distribution has a tail: in WildChat about 41% of conversations have more than one turn and 3.7% have more than 10 (WildChat §3).
  • The length view uses only WildChat Table 1, where all corpora were tokenized with the Llama-2 tokenizer. WildChat's user length has a standard deviation of 1609.18 tokens, so a small share of very long user messages can move the mean (interpretation; the paper reports no median).
  • CAMEL is absent: the paper reports 25,000 AI Society conversations and a 40-message cap, but no turn or token averages (CAMEL §4.1).
