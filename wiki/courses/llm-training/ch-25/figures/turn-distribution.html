What to look for.
  • Synthetic medians cluster at 3–7 turns. Baize (median 4) and UltraChat-Writing (median 4) are tight around the teacher's natural stopping point.
  • CAMEL has the longest tail among synthetic corpora because termination is semantic (<CAMEL_TASK_DONE>) not a turn cap — hard tasks genuinely run 15–20 rounds.
  • OpenAssistant and WildChat are the real-human baselines. OASST paths go to 20+ turns with a power-law tail; WildChat is bimodal — many 1–2 turn drops plus a heavy tail of 30+ turn sessions.
  • SODA has the shortest turns (~20 tokens) — Atomic-triple grounding elicits a chatty social-exchange register, not a Q&A register.
  • User-turn tokens < assistant-turn tokens in every corpus, but the gap is smaller in synthetic corpora (articulate synthetic users) than in WildChat (real users are short: ~20 tokens median vs. assistant ~180).
