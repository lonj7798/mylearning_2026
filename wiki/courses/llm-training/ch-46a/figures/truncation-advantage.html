Reference composition n = 8 with 2 successes, 4 verified failures, and 2 truncated rollouts reproduces the table in ch-46a §5: 0.857 / −0.286 under the first two policies, and 0.800 / −0.400 when truncated rollouts are dropped from the baseline. When every rollout that reached a verdict carries the same reward, all advantages are 0 and the task contributes nothing to the update; DeepSWE reports this condition as a high solve-none rate on SWE-Smith and SWE-Gym data (deepswe, §6). Advantage form and the masked terminations (max context, max environment steps, 20-minute generation timeout) are from the DeepSWE post §2.3 and the released script (deepswe-recipe); the post does not state whether masked trajectories stay in the baseline, which is why the second and third rows are both shown.
