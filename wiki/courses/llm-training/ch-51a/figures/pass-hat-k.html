pass^k = Etask[ C(c,k) / C(n,k) ]   and   pass@k = 1 − Etask[ C(n−c,k) / C(n,k) ]. A task with c < k contributes 0 to pass^k; a task with n − c < k contributes 1 to pass@k.

2. Both curves against k

Orange: pass^k, the share of tasks solved in every one of k trials — it falls with k. Blue: pass@k, the share solved in at least one of k trials — it rises with k. They meet only at k = 1. The vertical gap at a given k is the part of the pass@k score that a single run would not reproduce.
