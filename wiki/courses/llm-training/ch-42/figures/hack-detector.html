The proxy curve (orange) climbs during RL: that is what the policy optimizes. The true-reward curve (green) is a held-out probe — an external verifier, a human audit, a reference-guided evaluator — that is not itself being optimized. Divergence between the two is the Skalse 2022 signature: a proxy reward that increases while the true reward stagnates or falls. The red curve is the diagnostic — a cheap, continuous statistic that correlates with the hack and can be watched in training logs. See read.md §7 for the full pre-deployment checklist.
