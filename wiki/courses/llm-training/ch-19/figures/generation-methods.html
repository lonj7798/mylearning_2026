A. Self-Instruct ROUGE-L filter

A new instruction enters the pool only if its ROUGE-L similarity to every existing instruction is below 0.7. Enter a candidate and one pool instruction.

P = LCS(a,b)/|a| R = LCS(a,b)/|b| F = 2PR/(P+R) accept if F < 0.7

Tokenization here: lowercase, split on characters that are not letters or digits. Threshold and rule: Self-Instruct arXiv:2212.10560v2 §2.2. The Alpaca release computes the F-measure (generate_instruction.py @761dc5b L196); the Self-Instruct paper says "ROUGE-L similarity" without naming the variant. A low F only means few shared words in order; it does not show that the candidate asks for a different skill.

C. Length-controlled win rate

The length-controlled AlpacaEval regression predicts the judge's preference from a model term, a length term, and an instruction term, then removes the length term. Here the instruction term is set to 0.

Raw win rate
LC win rate
raw = logistic(θm − θb + φ·tanh(Δlen/std)) LC = logistic(θm − θb)

Formula: Length-Controlled AlpacaEval, arXiv:2404.04475v2 §3, Eq. 1–2. Slider values are chosen by the reader and are not fitted to any model. Measured effects from the paper: gpt4_1106_preview raw win rate 22.9%–64.3% across verbosity prompts versus 41.9%–51.6% length-controlled (§4.1); Spearman correlation with Chatbot Arena 0.94 → 0.98 (§4.2).
