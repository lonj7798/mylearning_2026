Table 3 prints D = 128.5B at V = 32K and D = 127.0B at V = 43K for the 2.3e21 budget; the fit gives values within 0.5% of those. The quadratic fit has its minimum near V ≈ 231K, so compression values above about 200K are not meaningful. Padding rule from OLMo-core tokenizer.py L77-82 ([[olmo-core-olmo3-configs]]).

Premiums from [[tokenizer-language-unfairness]] Table 1 (arXiv:2305.15425, FLORES-200, 2,000 parallel sentences). Tokens for the content = window × premium; share that fits = 1 / premium; relative compute = premium, because training compute per token is constant at fixed model size (C ≈ 6ND). These are derived quantities; the paper reports premiums, latency (Fig. 2), and context effects (§5.3), not downstream accuracy.
