One SFT run moves two numbers: target metric and broad-suite mean

Every point is one LLaMA-2-7B checkpoint from Table 1 of Shi et al. 2024 (arXiv:2405.14394v2). Horizontal axis: AlpacaEval 1.0 win rate, the target-style metric. Vertical axis: mean over 18 traditional NLP tasks in 6 categories, the broad suite. The dashed line is the base model's broad-suite mean, 49.32. Points below it are checkpoints that gained on the target metric and lost broad-suite score in the same run.
