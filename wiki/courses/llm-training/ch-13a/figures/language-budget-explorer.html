B. Tokenizer premium: content per window and tokens per unit of content

LanguagePremiumEnglish-token content per windowTokens for the same content (B)

Premium = tokens for a sentence in the language divided by tokens for its English translation, measured on the FLORES-200 parallel corpus. Content per window = window ÷ premium; tokens for the same content = budget × premium. The vertical line marks premium 1.0 (English). Languages whose text is more than 10% UNK for a tokenizer are not listed by the source. FLORES-200 contains English-centric names, which the authors note may favor English. Source: Petrov et al., arXiv:2305.15425, Tables 1, 4, 5 (chapter excerpt tokenizer-language-unfairness).
