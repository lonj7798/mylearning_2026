2. fp16 loss scaling

A gradient entry g is computed in fp16. Multiplying the loss by S multiplies every gradient by S; the gradients are divided by S in fp32 before clipping and the optimizer step. If the largest gradient times S exceeds 65,504, fp16 produces an infinity and the step is skipped.

Mechanism: Micikevicius et al., Mixed Precision Training, arXiv:1710.03740 §3.2 (loss scaling, unscaling before gradient clipping, skipping updates on overflow; constant scale factors from 8 to 32K in that paper). Update rule shown for the dynamic case uses PyTorch 2.12.0 GradScaler defaults (torch/amp/grad_scaler.py L123-131): init_scale 216, backoff_factor 0.5, growth_factor 2.0, growth_interval 2000. The default g = 10-7.83 is below fp16's smallest subnormal, 2-24.

3. FP8 E4M3: one scale per tensor versus one scale per block

Eight values form two groups of four. Each group is scaled by s = 448 / max|x| before rounding to E4M3 and divided by s afterwards. With one scale for the whole tensor, the outlier in group B sets s for group A as well.
