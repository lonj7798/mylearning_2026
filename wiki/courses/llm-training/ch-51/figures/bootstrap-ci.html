Precision of one score, and of a difference

Companion to ch-51. Panel 1 shows how the 95% halfwidth of a single score splits between the item-sampling term, which falls as 1/sqrt(n), and the run-to-run term, which does not. Panel 2 generates paired per-item scores at a chosen correlation and compares the paired interval (Eq. 7 of [[adding-error-bars-evals]]) with the unpaired one (Eq. 5) and with the sign test. All computation runs in the browser; no data is loaded from anywhere.

Panel 1 — where the halfwidth of one score comes from

Item term: 1.96·sqrt(p(1−p)/n)·(1 + (2/K − 2/1)·0 + …) is not used here; the generation effect is applied as Var(µ̂|K) = (Var(x) + E[σ²]/K)/n with Var(x) = p(1−p)/2 and E[σ²] = p(1−p)/2 as an illustrative split of the binary variance (labelled illustrative because no source measures this split for a given benchmark). Run term: 1.96·σ_run/sqrt(R), with σ_run defaulting to 0.80 pp, the ARC-C seed standard deviation measured over 10 identically trained 7B models in Madaan et al. 2024, Table 1. Dashed markers: HumanEval n=164, MATH500 n=500, GSM8K n=1319, MMLU n=14042.
