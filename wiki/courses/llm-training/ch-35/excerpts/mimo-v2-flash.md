## Model (Abstract)
MoE with 309B total and 15B active parameters; pre-trained on 27T tokens at 32k context, extended to 256k.

## Post-training stages (§4.1, Figure 3)
1. **SFT.** "Millions of training samples spanning diverse domains, including general conversation, reasoning, coding, and agent tasks. These samples cover both thinking and non-thinking modes, with responses generated by our in-house domain-specialized model checkpoints" (§4.2). Settings: cosine LR 5.0e-5 → 5.0e-6, batch 128, AdamW ε 1.0e-8, MoE expert-bias update rate 1.0e-4, sequence auxiliary loss coefficient 1.0e-6. The number of parameters with zero gradients ("num-zeros") is monitored: rising indicates deteriorating expert load balance, falling indicates overfitting (§4.2).
2. **Domain-specialized training.** Teachers trained by independent RL for search, coding, general tool use, math reasoning, general reasoning, and safety (§4.1).
3. **Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD).** "The student model samples from its own evolving distribution and receives token-level supervision from domain-specific teachers through KL divergence rewards" (§4.1).

## Objective (§4.4)
- Reverse KL: L_reverse-KL(θ) = −E_{x∼D, y_t∼π_θ} [ log( π_domain_x(y_t | x, y_<t) / π_θ(y_t | x, y_<t) ) ] (Eq. 5), where π_domain_x is the teacher for the domain of prompt x.
- Surrogate: L_MOPD(θ) = −E_{x, y∼μ_θ} [ (1/|y|) Σ_t w_t Â_MOPD,t log π_θ(y_t | x, y_<t) ] (Eq. 7). μ_θ is the inference-engine sampler; w_t = sg[π_θ/μ_θ] when ε_low ≤ π_θ/μ_θ ≤ ε_high and 0 otherwise; Â_MOPD,t = sg[ log(π_domain_x / π_θ) ] (Eq. 8).
- Default combination with outcome-reward advantages: Â_MOPD,t = sg[log(π_domain_x / π_θ)] + α Â_ORM (Eq. 9). α, ε_low, ε_high are not printed.
