    B. Loss aggregation across two samples

    Formulas from Tülu 3 (arXiv:2411.15124v5 §4.3.2, Eqs. 1-2): one forward pass L = (l₁ + l₂)/(n₁ + n₂); gradient accumulation with one sample per micro-batch L = (l₁/n₁ + l₂/n₂)/2; sum loss L = l₁ + l₂, where lₖ is the summed token loss of sample k. Tülu 3 reports that it used a sum loss and that this "requires an adjustment to learning rates". Default inputs reproduce the worked example in read.md §3.

(arXiv:2405.14394v2 Eq. 4), whose main results (Table 1) use single-turn data; the paper does not describe how earlier turns are treated in multi-turn data.
