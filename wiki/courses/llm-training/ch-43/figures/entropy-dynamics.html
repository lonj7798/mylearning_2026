Update rule: Δz_j = η·A·(1[j = a] − π_j), the exact gradient of log π_a with respect to the logits, then a softmax. The first-order prediction is Lemma 1 of entropy-mechanism-llm-rl (arXiv:2505.22617v1 §3.1): ΔH ≈ −Cov_{a~π}(log π(a), Δz_a). The comparison point named in the chapter is the policy's mean log-probability, Σ_j π_j log π_j = −H.
