Entropy Dynamics and KL Estimator Variance

Companion figure for ch-43. Panel 1: policy entropy H(π) vs training step under four interventions; the red dashed line is the H < 0.1 collapse threshold from [[entropy-mechanism-llm-rl]]. Panel 2: per-sample variance of the k1 / k2 / k3 KL estimators from [[john-schulman-kl-tricks]] as you vary the gap between q (current policy) and p (reference). Curves are illustrative and match the shapes attested in the papers; absolute numbers are pedagogical.

Panel 2 — k1 / k2 / k3 estimator variance

k1 = −log r   k2 = ½·(log r)²   k3 = r − 1 − log r
Scatter: 1000 single-sample KL estimates for Bernoulli-ish q and p with log(p/q) ~ Normal(ε, 0.5). True KL(q‖p) is roughly ε when ε is small. Note: k1 crosses zero; k2 and k3 never do. As ε grows, k1's variance explodes (tail draws give huge |log r|); k2 stays close to k3 near r=1 but biases away; k3 keeps both non-negativity and low variance in the operating regime of PPO/GRPO after a few steps.
