Larger K buys more selection per round but invites reward-model overoptimization past K=64 (see best-of-n.md). Smaller top-fraction keeps only the cleanest samples but wastes generation budget. Llama-2 uses K=10 top-1; Llama-3 uses K=10-30 top-(K/4); ReST-EM uses K=32 with a diversity cap.
RSFT-only saturates fastest (ReST-EM 2-iter ceiling). DPO-only lifts slower per round but is less likely to plateau. Alternation (green) approximates the Llama-2 / Tulu-3 multi-stage gain - each RSFT round resets the reference for the next DPO.
