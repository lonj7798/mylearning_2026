1. Which problems produce accepted samples

A problem with per-sample success probability p, sampled K times, yields at least one accepted sample with probability pass@K = 1 − (1 − p)K. The number of accepted samples X follows Binomial(K, p); with a per-problem cap c, the kept count is min(X, c).

2. Per-sample gradient weights under different update rules

Each cell is one sampled response. Click a cell to switch its reward between 1 (correct) and 0 (incorrect). The number under the reward is the coefficient wij that multiplies ∇θ log πθ(yij | xi) in one gradient step taken at the sampling policy.

Promptrewardssum of positive weightsum of negative weightshare of all positive weight

Rules. (1) RSFT: w = r / |A|, A = accepted samples (after the cap). (2) REINFORCE with r ∈ {0,1}, baseline 0: w = r / (M·n); rule 1 equals rule 2 times 1/a, a = |A| / (M·n) ([[star]] Eq. 2; [[rest-em]] Algorithm 1). (3) REINFORCE with r ∈ {−1,+1}, baseline 0: w = (2r − 1) / (M·n). (4) Group-mean baseline: w = (r − meani) / (M·n). (5) GRPO-style: w = (r − meani) / stdi / (M·n), population standard deviation, w = 0 when stdi = 0. (6) Reinforce-Rej: rule 3 after removing prompts whose samples are all correct or all incorrect, normalized over the remaining prompts ([[raft-reinforce-rej-minimalist]] §5.1). Token-level averaging, importance ratios, and clipping are omitted. M = 3 prompts.
