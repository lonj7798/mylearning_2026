B — staleness & vllm_kl (proxy)

vllm_kl proxy is k · 0.04 · leak(mode). leak is 1.0 for off, 0.2 for tis (matches PolicyLoss.forward clip to [0.5, 2.0]), 0.05 for icepop (per-token mask). Clipfrac pegs at 1 once vllm_kl > 0.10 — the canary in [[async-rollout]] §Failure signature.

What this simulates vs what is a cartoon

Attested numbers: throughput ratio 1.6–2.0× at sync→async on 8×H100 / 7B ([[async-rollout]] Figure 5); queue depth d=1..2 as the production default (both OpenRLHF and verl); vllm_kl > 0.1 with clipfrac pegged as the failure signature; clip range [0.5, 2.0] on the IS weight from [[openrlhf-ppo]] vllm_is_truncated_threshold.
Cartoons: the 0.04 nat/step drift constant is illustrative; real drift depends on LR · token entropy. Dot colors are simulated staleness, not real tokens. The "partial rollout" mode here just halves the in-flight penalty — the real [[verl-rollout]] implementation interrupts decoding mid-sequence via the priority queue.
