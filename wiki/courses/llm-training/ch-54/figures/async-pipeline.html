Admission rule, evaluated

The rule is ⌊(N_r − 1)/B⌋ ≤ i + η, where N_r is the cumulative number of generated trajectories, i the current policy version, and B the training batch size in prompts. Generation requests beyond the bound are rejected until the trainer advances, so the generator can run at most η + 1 batches ahead however fast it is. A generator faster than that bound idles; a generator slower than the trainer never reaches the bound and the run is generation-limited.
