MinHash banding: P(candidate | s) = 1 − (1 − sb)r

s is the Jaccard index of the two documents' 5-gram sets, b the number of hashes per bucket, and r the number of buckets. Lee et al. 2021 §4.2 use k = 9,000 hashes as r = 450 buckets of b = 20 hashes, then confirm candidates with Jaccard > 0.8 and edit similarity > 0.8. Reading the curve: the index is a candidate generator, so it should propose nearly every pair above the confirmation threshold of 0.8 and few pairs below it. With b = 20 and r = 450 the probability at s = 0.8 is 0.995 and at s = 0.5 is 0.0004. With the two numbers exchanged the midpoint moves to (1/20)1/450 = 0.993 and a pair at s = 0.9 is proposed with probability below 1e−15, so almost every true near-duplicate is missed and the gate reports a near-zero overlap rate on every task. Library APIs commonly take the pair as (bands, rows_per_band), which reverses the paper's "buckets of hashes" wording; checking the value at s = 0.8 catches the mistake in one line.
