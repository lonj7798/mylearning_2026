Superseded figure: SFT ablation cube

This page previously showed a 2×2×2 cube of packing, prompt masking, and NEFTune with illustrative MT-Bench scores. The 2026-09 revision of ch-36 removed that figure because its justifications cited results that the sources do not contain:

  • Shi et al. (arXiv:2405.14394v2) do not report a 1.5-2.0 point MT-Bench gain for response-only loss; their Table 2 is a BLEU table. In their Table 1, the MT-Bench difference of response-only loss minus loss on instructions ranges from −0.68 to +0.17 across seven datasets, and adding loss on instruction tokens raised the 18-task NLP mean on all seven.
  • The Tülu 3 report (arXiv:2411.15124v5) contains no packing ablation and does not mention NEFTune.
  • The corner scores were not measurements.

The revised lab uses forgetting-ci-planner.html, which computes paired and seed-inclusive intervals for base-versus-SFT differences.
