Forgetting interval planner

Set a suite size, the base model's correct count, and how many items an SFT checkpoint lost and gained. The planner computes the forgetting delta and four 95% intervals: unpaired, paired over items, paired with seed variance, and the last one widened for multiple comparisons. Defaults reproduce the GSM8K example in ch-36 §6.1.

Inputs

Intervals for SFT − base (accuracy points)
