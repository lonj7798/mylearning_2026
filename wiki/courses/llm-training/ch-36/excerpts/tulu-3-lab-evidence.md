**Paper:** Lambert, Morrison, Pyatkin, Huang, Ivison, Brahman, et al. (Allen Institute for AI; University of Washington), "Tülu 3: Pushing Frontiers in Open Language Model Post-Training". arXiv v1 2024-11; read at v5 (2025-04-14). Source type: official technical report.

The number of runs per ablation is not reported.

§7.4.1: "the data choices generalize on average ... our choices overfit to the development evaluations in Precise Instruction Following, and to some extent in Knowledge Recall and Reasoning." Persona IF was generated to cover "25 different constraint types defined in IFEval benchmark" (§3.1.2). IFEval-OOD has 52 constraints in six categories (§7.3.1).

## SFT training settings (§4.3, Table 11)

8B: LR 5×10⁻⁶, linear schedule, effective batch 128, max token length 4,096, warmup ratio 0.03, 2 epochs. 70B: LR 2×10⁻⁶, otherwise the same. "The final 8B model is trained on 32 GPUs for 6 hours and the 70B model was trained on 64 GPUs for 50 hours" (§4.3). Values "we found after a hyperparameter search" (§4.3). §4.3.2: a sum loss instead of a mean loss; on Llama 3.0 with the Tülu 2 mixture, "using a sum loss with a learning rate of 5.00E-06 worked best" and "training for longer did not yield further improvements, and so used 2 epochs" (Figs. 5-6, curves without numeric labels).

## Seeds (§4.3.1, Table 14)

8B SFT seeds 42, 123, 456, 789, 1011: averages 59.9, 60.1, 59.8, 59.8, 59.8; best soup (42 & 123) 60.2. 70B seeds 42, 123, 456: 71.8, 70.0, 72.6; best soup (123 & 456) 72.5. "SFT performance noticeably varies based on the seed, highlighting the importance of multiple training runs"; the best single run was used as the final SFT model.

## Mixture counts (Table 7) and data-size trend (Fig. 4)

SFT prompts by category (sums of Table 7 rows): General 116,872 (Hardcoded 240, OpenAssistant 7,132, No Robots 9,500, WildChat 100,000); Knowledge recall 104,982 (FLAN v2 89,982, SciRIFF 10,000, TableGPT 5,000); Math reasoning 334,252 (Persona MATH 149,960, Persona GSM 49,980, Persona Algebra 20,000, OpenMathInstruct 2 50,000, NuminaMath-TIR 64,312); Coding 142,275 (Persona Python 34,999, Evol CodeAlpaca 107,276); Safety and non-compliance 110,983 (CoCoNot 10,983, WildJailbreak 50,000, WildGuardMix 50,000); Multilingual 100,000 (Aya); Precise IF 29,980 (Persona IF). Total 939,344. Persona math and coding data (§3.1.2): problems generated by zero-shot prompting GPT-4o with a persona from about 250K Persona Hub personas; math solutions by GPT-4o and Python programs by claude-3-5-sonnet. Fig. 4 (stratified subsamples 5%-100%): average performance improves with more SFT data; "TruthfulQA performance actually drops as the amount of data in the mix increases" (§4.2; no numeric labels).

## Decontamination (§3.2)

8-gram matching on prompts (user turns). A test token matches if the test and training instance share an 8-gram containing it; a test instance overlaps a training instance if more than 50% of its tokens match that one instance. A training set is contaminated if it overlaps more than 2% of the instances of any development or unseen evaluation. Sets contaminated with unseen evaluations are removed; for development evaluations the whole set is removed if performance is not significantly affected, otherwise the matching instances are removed. Embedding-based matching was tested and not used because distributional similarity could not be separated from paraphrase.

## Held-out coding subset (§7.3)

BigCodeBench: "hard subset" of 148 of 1,140 instances, instruct formulation, calibrated score. IFEval in the development suite is scored as prompt-level loose accuracy with greedy decoding (§7.2).

## Chat template (§4.3.1, Table 13)

Intermediate SFT mix on Llama 3.0, average score by template: Tülu (replace \n with eos) 53.0; Zephyr 52.9; Tülu 3 (no \n) 52.8; Tülu 2 52.6; Llama 3 51.6.
