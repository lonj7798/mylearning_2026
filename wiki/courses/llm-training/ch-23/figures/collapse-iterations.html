Mechanism. At each generation, draw N samples from pn; if verifier pass-rate v > 0, reject a sample with probability v · d(x, true_support) (samples far from true support more likely rejected); mix surviving samples with an a-fraction re-sampled from the true distribution; refit mixture weights and means on the mixed batch. The recursion Var[ŵk] ≈ n·wk(1-wk)/N drives the low-weight components to zero first; once a component hits zero, it is irretrievable.
