Chapter 48 figures — LSH banding and estimated performance gain

Two calculators for the two numbers a contamination memo reports: which pairs a near-duplicate detector will propose, and how many benchmark points the flagged items are worth.

Candidate probability P(s) = 1 − (1 − sb)r

b = hashes that must all agree inside one bucket. r = number of buckets. s = Jaccard similarity of the shingle sets.

50% crossing of this curve: s ≈ 0.723
