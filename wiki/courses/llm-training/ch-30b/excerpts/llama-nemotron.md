# Excerpt: Llama-Nemotron: Efficient Reasoning Models

- **Authors:** NVIDIA (byline)
- **Year:** arXiv v1 2025-05; v5 2025-09-09
- **Checked against:** arXiv:2505.00949v5 (PDF, §1-§7), 2026-09-15
- **Why this excerpt exists:** ch-30b cites Llama-Nemotron for mixing reasoning-on and reasoning-off data in one SFT set. The library card `llama-nemotron` was planned but not present when the chapter was written. This report is different from the Nemotron 3 card `nemotron-ultra`.

## Models
- LN-Nano (8B, Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1), LN-Super (49B, Llama-3.3-Nemotron-Super-49B-v1), LN-Ultra (253B, Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Ultra-253B-v1) (§1).

## Reasoning toggle in the data (§3, §3.2, §4.1)
- Reasoning samples carry the system instruction "detailed thinking on"; non-reasoning samples carry "detailed thinking off" (§3).
- Paired data: prompts are sampled from the reasoning dataset and given a second, non-reasoning response generated by Llama-3.1-Nemotron-70B-Instruct (general domain) or Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct (other domains); responses are filtered by ground-truth answers or reward models (§3.2).
- "For most settings, training batches mix reasoning and non-reasoning data, where prompts are paired with responses conditioned on the respective system instruction" (§4.1).
- "Models require higher learning rates to effectively learn from long reasoning traces, especially due to sequence-length-dependent token loss averaging" (§4.1).

## Model-specific SFT (§4.2)
- LN-Nano: three stages, global batch 256, sequence packing at 32k. Stage 1: reasoning data only (code, math, science), LR 1e-4, four epochs; "This prevents failure modes such as repetitive completions." Stage 2: non-reasoning data mixed with reasoning samples. Stage 3: a smaller blend of chat, instruction following, and tool calling.
- LN-Super: full SFT set, one epoch, fixed LR 5e-6, sequence length 16k, global batch 256. Smaller runs suggested gains up to 3-4 epochs at LR 5e-5 (no table).
- LN-Ultra: sequence packing at 24k, global batch 256, linear warmup to 1e-5 over 10% then cosine decay to 1e-6; gradient explosions after the first epoch required resumption with reinitialized optimizer states.

## Results by mode (Tables 3-4; evaluation settings §7.1)
- Reasoning-on evaluation uses temperature 0.6 and top-p 0.95; reasoning-off uses greedy decoding; up to 16 completions per prompt, average pass@1 (§7.1).
- LN-Nano (on | off): AIME24 61.3 | 3.0; MATH500 95.4 | 36.6; BFCL V2 Live 63.9 | 63.6; IFEval 79.29 | 82.1 (Table 3).
- LN-Super-SFT (on | off): IFEval 81.9 | 83.0; released LN-Super (after the IFEval RL run, online-RPO RLHF, and model merging, §6-§7.3): 89.2 | 89.0; Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct 92.1 (Table 4). The report states that "reasoning-focused SFT causes a noticeable drop in IFEval scores" and that a dedicated IFEval RL run was added (§7.3).
- The report also states a trade-off between IFEval and Arena-Hard during LN-Super training, handled with model merging and a Pareto-frontier checkpoint; merging was not adopted for the other models "due to mixed outcomes" (§7.3).
- For LN-Nano, upsampling chemistry-related STEM data raised GPQA-Diamond after early runs were weak on chemistry questions (§7.2; no numbers).

## Limits
- No controlled ablation of the reasoning-on share or of paired versus unpaired data is reported.
- Checkpoints are selected on a subset of reasoning benchmarks (§7.1), so reasoning-off scores are not the selection target.
