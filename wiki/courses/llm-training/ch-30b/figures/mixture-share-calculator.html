Mixture share calculator: examples, size units, and loss weight

A mixture share depends on its unit. This page converts one mixture into four shares: raw examples, sampled examples after per-dataset multipliers, total size (tokens or rounds), and loss tokens. The loss-aggregation setting marks which share equals each domain's weight in the SFT loss. Values in every cell can be edited.
