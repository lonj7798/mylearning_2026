Formulas: SEpaired = sqrt( Σ(di − d̄)² / (n − 1) / n ) with di ∈ {−1, 0, +1}; SEunpaired = sqrt( pref(1 − pref)/n + pft(1 − pft)/n ); interval = delta ± 1.96 × SE. Equation numbers refer to Miller, "Adding Error Bars to Evals", arXiv:2411.00640v1 (§2.1, §4.1, §4.2). Agreement here is the share of items with the same correct/wrong outcome for both checkpoints. Kalajdzievski (arXiv:2401.05605v1 §3.1) reports a related quantity on ARC-Challenge, the share of items where the fine-tuned model gives the reference model's answer; on multiple-choice items that answer agreement is at most the outcome agreement shown here, because two different wrong answers count as the same outcome. The preset counts are constructed examples from ch-30a §1, not data from a source. The normal approximation is less accurate when few items change.
