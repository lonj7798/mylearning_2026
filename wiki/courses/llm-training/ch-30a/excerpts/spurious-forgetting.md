# Excerpt: Spurious Forgetting in Continual Learning of Language Models

**Authors:** Junhao Zheng, Xidi Cai, Shengjie Qiu, Qianli Ma (South China University of Technology)
**Version read:** arXiv:2501.13453v1 (23 Jan 2025), "Published as a conference paper at ICLR 2025".
**Status:** no library card existed for this slug on 2026-09-15; quotes checked against the v1 PDF text.

## Hypothesis (§1)
"Task Performance = Task Alignment + Underlying Knowledge". Spurious forgetting is a performance drop caused by a loss of task alignment rather than a loss of knowledge.

## Safety example (§2)
"Initially, the safety performance of LLaMa-2-7B-Chat is 100% ... after training the model on the 10 Identity Shifting Instances for 10 epochs, the safety performance drops to 0%." Ten harmful instructions with refusals generated by the pre-attack model, trained for ten epochs, raise safety "from 0% to approximately 99%." Safety performance is "100% minus the jailbreak rate" on AdvBench.

## Controlled experiment (§3.1-3.2)
Biography dataset: 200,000 synthetic individuals with six attributes. Pretraining on 100,000 individuals, QA fine-tuning on the same individuals (Task 0), then Task 1 with 20,000 new individuals. LR 1×10⁻³ (pretraining) and 5×10⁻⁶ (fine-tuning); 80K and 62.5K steps.
- Task 0 accuracy drops "from nearly 100% to around 10% within the initial 150 optimization steps."
- Recovery test: fine-tune on half of Task 0 data for one epoch, evaluate on the other half. "recovery performance remains nearly 100% during the first 150 steps of training on Task 1, decreasing slightly to 96% by the end of Task 1."

## Mechanism claims (§3.4-3.5, §4)
The first 150 steps of Task 1 update weights "in a space close to that of Task 0"; later steps update "a distinctly different space, particularly affecting the bottom layers, including input embeddings." Proposition 4.9 bounds the accumulated output shift by L ε_Δ (1 + δ)^{L−1} ‖X_0‖, which the authors use to motivate freezing bottom layers.

## Mitigation results
- Freeze on Biography: "improving task accuracy in sequential fine-tuning (SEQ) from 11% to 44%, while other techniques peaked at 22%" (§1).
- Table 2, safety alignment (LLaMa-2-7B-Chat, AOA): jailbreak rate SEQ 99.80±0.20; Freeze (3 layers) 79.61±6.53; Freeze (6 layers) 1.15±0.16. The authors state the SA result is "not intended to demonstrate that Freeze is a defence method to jailbreak attacks."
- "a significant performance gap remains between Freeze and data replay" (§5.2).
