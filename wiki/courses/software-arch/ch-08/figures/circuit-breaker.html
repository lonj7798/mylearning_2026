Circuit Breaker — interactive state machine

You are the agent making calls to one flaky dependency. Click Send request to fire a call. Toggle the dependency down to simulate a vendor outage, then keep sending and watch the breaker trip OPEN and start failing fast (rejecting before the network even tries). Heal the dependency and let the cooldown elapse to reach HALF-OPEN, where a single probe decides whether to close again. The whole point: while OPEN, calls cost ~0ms and never touch the dead vendor.
