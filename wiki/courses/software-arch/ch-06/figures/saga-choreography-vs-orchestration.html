Saga: 3-Step Order — Choreography vs Orchestration

A saga replaces one ACID transaction with three local transactions glued together. There is no rollback — failures run compensating transactions in reverse. Toggle the coordination style, then inject a failure and watch the lost-isolation window.
Order Orchestrator (central coordinator)
Idle — will issue commands and await replies.

1. Inventory

reserve(item)  |  compensate: release(item)
stock: 5 — reserved: 0

2. Payment

charge(card)  |  compensate: refund(card)
balance: $0 — charged: $0

3. Order

confirm(order)  |  compensate: cancel(order)
order: PENDING
committed local txn running failed step compensated (semantically undone)
