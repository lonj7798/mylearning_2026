Soft Targets & Temperature — the "dark knowledge" in a teacher

A teacher classifies a handwritten 2. At temperature T=1 it is nearly one-hot — almost a hard label. Raise T and the teacher's belief that a 2 looks a bit like a 3 or 7 (and nothing like an 8) emerges. That similarity structure over the wrong classes is the extra signal a hard label throws away.
