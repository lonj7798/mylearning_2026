Compounding Error: why off-policy training punishes long sequences

Behavioral cloning (off-policy imitation) accrues error that grows as O(ε·T²) in the horizon T — each early mistake drifts the model into states it was never trained on. On-policy data collection makes it O(ε·T). Set the per-step error ε and slide the horizon T; the ratio of the two is exactly T.
