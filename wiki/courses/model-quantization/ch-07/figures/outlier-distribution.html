LLM Activation Outliers — The 0.1% That Breaks INT8

Transformers at 6.7B+ params develop a handful of activation channels with magnitudes 10–100× larger than the bulk. Naïve per-tensor INT8 quantization loses 5+ PPL. LLM.int8's fix: route those columns through FP16, keep the rest in INT8.

The kill shot. When max |x| is set by an outlier of magnitude 60, the INT8 step size becomes 60/127 ≈ 0.47. The bulk of activations live in [−3, 3], so they get only ~13 distinct INT8 levels instead of 256 — catastrophic precision loss in the region that carries 90% of the gradient signal. LLM.int8 (Dettmers 2022) splits the GEMM: outlier columns go through FP16, bulk columns stay INT8 with a tight range. ~0.1% of columns are touched. Per-token / per-channel scaling (Q8BERT, SmoothQuant) attack the same problem from a different angle — see [[ch-07]] body for the comparison.
