SmoothQuant — Activation → Weight Difficulty Migration

Per-channel rescale sj = max(|Xj|)α / max(|Wj|)(1−α) shifts quantization difficulty from outlier-rich activations into well-behaved weights. Y = (X · diag(s)−1) · (diag(s) · W) = X · W

Why it works. Activations have a handful of outlier channels that blow up the INT8 step size; weights don't. SmoothQuant divides X by a per-channel scale and multiplies W by the same scale — algebraically identical (Y = X · W), but it pulls the outlier mass out of X and pushes a small, even amount into W. There's no extra compute at inference (fuse s into the previous LayerNorm); only a one-time weight rewrite. α = 0.5 is the empirical sweet spot — α=0 reverts to vanilla quantization (catastrophic for X), α=1 over-migrates and explodes W.
