Block-MSE loss vs training step

The grid-search → gradient leap. AWQ picks a single clipping ratio per layer by sweeping a discrete grid (typically 20 candidates), then locks it in. OmniQuant treats αmin and αmax as continuous learnable parameters with a soft sigmoid parameterization and lets gradient descent find the joint optimum across every weight column simultaneously. Same data, same calibration set — but the search space is continuous and joint. Empirically: ~30% lower perplexity at W3A16 over AWQ, ~1 hour calibration on a single A100 for Llama-7B.
