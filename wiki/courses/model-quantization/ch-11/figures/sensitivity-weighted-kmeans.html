SqueezeLLM — Sensitivity-Weighted k-Means Centroids

Standard k-means minimizes Σ (w − c)²; SqueezeLLM minimizes Σ Fw · (w − c)² where Fw is the Fisher information. High-Fisher (high-sensitivity) values attract more centroids.

The reframing. Uniform k-means treats every weight as equally important — it shoves more centroids toward the fat middle of the Gaussian where most mass lives. SqueezeLLM observes that the few outlier weights carry disproportionate gradient signal (high Fisher), so wasting them by quantizing to the nearest bulk centroid is catastrophic. Weighting the k-means objective by Fw pulls centroids toward those rare-but-important weights. SpQR takes the extreme view: pull the top ~0.5% out entirely, store them FP16 in a sparse matrix, and 4-bit the rest aggressively. Outliers are signal, not noise.
