OBS update rule
for q in 0..N:
  w_q = W[:, q]
  ŵ_q = quant(w_q)
  e = (w_q − ŵ_q) / [H⁻¹]_{q,q}
  δ_F = − e · [H⁻¹]_{q,F}
  W[:, F] += δ_F

Quantizing column q creates error e. OBS spreads −e across the remaining columns F = {q+1, ..., N−1}, weighted by the corresponding row of H⁻¹. This minimizes ‖WX − ŴX‖² in closed form.

Why this matters. OBS (Hassibi & Stork 1993) gives the optimal closed-form weight update given the layer's Hessian. The catch: it's O(N³) per layer and needs the full H⁻¹ in memory, which is intractable for transformer columns of size 10k+. GPTQ's contribution is to process columns in Cholesky blocks so the algorithm becomes compute-bound instead of memory-bound — see [[ch-08]] for the speedup.
