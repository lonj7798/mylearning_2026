PolarQuant — Angle Distribution Before vs After Preconditioning

1000 random 16-dimensional heavy-tailed vectors. The angle θ between consecutive coordinate pairs is data-dependent before preconditioning and converges to a closed-form Beta-like density after a random orthogonal (Hadamard) transform — independent of the input distribution.

  heavy-tailed mix: 50% Gaussian + 50% Cauchy, d = 16

Caption. After random preconditioning, the angle distribution converges to a closed-form Beta density that depends only on dimension d. This is why PolarQuant needs no calibration — the optimal angle quantizer is computable in advance. Click "resample inputs" repeatedly: the Stage 1 histogram jitters and reshapes with each draw; the Stage 2 histogram stays pinned to the red analytical curve. That stability is the data-oblivious thesis.
