Marlin W4A16 GEMM — Three-Stage Pipeline

Three warps execute a double-buffered pipeline. At iteration k, warp 0 issues an async copy for tile k+2, warp 1 dequantizes tile k+1, warp 2 runs the MMA for tile k. Tensor cores stay fed.

Caption. Marlin's three-stage pipeline + double-buffered shared memory + warp specialization is what gets W4A16 GEMM to near-FP16 throughput at low batch sizes. This is the bridge from GPTQ paper to production speedup. Watch the warmup: iterations 0–1 have idle stages because the pipeline hasn't yet filled; from iteration 2 onward all three warps are busy every tick and the throughput counter ramps to the steady-state ~300 TFLOPS on a single Hopper SM partition (cluster-wide totals are higher).
