Straight-Through Estimator (STE)

Forward pass uses the true rounding q(x) = round(x/Δ)·Δ. The true derivative is zero almost everywhere with infinite spikes at step boundaries — useless for SGD. STE replaces it with the identity 1, letting gradient flow straight through the quantizer.

Δ = 1.0
Forward q(x) Identity y=x reference STE backward (=1) True backward (δ-spikes)
The bias is bounded. At step boundaries, true gradient is an infinite spike whose mass equals Δ; STE replaces this with a constant 1. The per-step bias is at most Δ in expectation, and over thousands of training steps these biases average toward zero while the optimizer still receives a useful, non-zero signal. The whole reason QAT works is this controlled lie.
