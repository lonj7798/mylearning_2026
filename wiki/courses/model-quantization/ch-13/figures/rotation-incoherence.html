QuIP Rotation — Making a Weight Matrix Incoherent

Start with a heavy-tailed weight matrix W (outlier rows and columns). Apply a random orthogonal rotation W' = U · W · V built from randomized Hadamard transforms. Same matrix, same information — but the energy is now spread uniformly, and per-weight INT4 quantization becomes cheap.

QuIP's incoherence processing. A few outlier rows/columns make per-tensor INT4 useless: the global scale has to span the largest weight, wasting bits on every "normal" weight. Rotating into a random orthogonal frame turns the distribution Gaussian-like (CLT in action — every output is a sum of many independent contributions), the max drops by 3–10×, kurtosis collapses, and INT4 round-to-nearest produces an error roughly 3–5× smaller. Same data, different basis, much cheaper quantization. The rotation is undone after the matmul, so quality is preserved end-to-end.
