Integer-Only Requantization Pipeline

Jacob 2018 mobile inference recipe: FP32 inputs are quantized, the GEMM runs in INT8/INT32, and a single fixed-point scalar M = M₀ · 2⁻ⁿ requantizes the accumulator. Every modern mobile-CPU LLM kernel descends from this diagram.

S_w = 0.0100
S_x = 0.0316
S_y = 0.3162
M = S_w · S_x / S_y— Fixed-point: M ≈ M₀ · 2⁻ⁿ— M₀ (INT32 in [2³⁰, 2³¹))— n (right-shift amount)— Reconstructed M̃ = M₀ · 2⁻ⁿ— Relative error |M̃ − M| / M—
The trick. The only floating-point number in this kernel — M — is always in (0, 1) for typical scales. Jacob shows you can represent it as M₀ · 2⁻ⁿ with M₀ ∈ [2³⁰, 2³¹). The whole requant becomes a single 32-bit multiply followed by a rounded right-shift. No FPU needed — the entire forward pass is integer arithmetic, which is why this fits on a microcontroller. The activation/weight scales themselves come from per-tensor calibration (covered in [[ch-07]] for LLMs where this naïve recipe breaks).
