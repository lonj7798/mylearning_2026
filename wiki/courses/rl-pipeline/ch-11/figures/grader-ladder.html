2. Hybrid grader calculator: rules first, model verifier on rule-rejected responses

For a binary reward, the expected reward of a correct response is the grader's true-positive rate (TPR) and of an incorrect response its false-positive rate (FPR). The expected reward gap TPR − FPR is the signal a group-relative advantage can use; a grader with TPR − FPR = 0 gives no signal.
