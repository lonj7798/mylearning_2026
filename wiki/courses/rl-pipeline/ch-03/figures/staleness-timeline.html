How to read this

Within one trajectory, staleness runs from about 23 learner steps (first turns, step-104 weights) down to about 3 (last turns, step-124 weights). The sampler lag is never more than 3 of those steps. The rest comes from the rollout's own duration and, for the faster siblings, from waiting hours for the slowest one. The controller queue (12 s) is negligible because Rocket keeps the learner overallocated.
