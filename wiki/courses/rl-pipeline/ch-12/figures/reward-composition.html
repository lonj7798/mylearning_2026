Reward composition and prompt selection

Companion to ch-12 §2, §3 and §7. Part 1 computes one GRPO group's rewards and advantages under three combination rules: a weighted sum, MAI's lexicographic rule, and MAI's gated rule, as ch-12 §3 states them. Part 2 applies pass-rate filters to a set of prompts across epochs. All rollouts, prompts and default weights marked illustrative are constructed for this figure. Sourced numbers carry their source.

(a) Weighted sum: R = wc·correct + wf·format − wlen·|y|/ℓmax + ws·compliant + wq·quality/5

(b) Lexicographic (MAI Sec. 3.4.1): the first term in priority order that is not tied within the group is the reward; lower terms are ignored

Each term is used on its own raw scale (correct 0/1, format 0/1, compliant 0/1, quality 1–5, length −|y|/ℓmax). Weights play no role, which is why the rule is "invariant to the absolute scale of each reward" (MAI Sec. 3.4.1). Use ← to raise a term's priority.

(c) Gated (MAI Sec. 3.4.1, 3.4.3): rollouts that fail the gate get the minimum reward; the others get the weighted mixture of the remaining terms

2. Prompt selection by pass-rate window

Each row is one prompt with k correct out of G = 8 rollouts under the current policy (illustrative). With a binary reward, a group with k = 0 or k = G has zero advantage for every rollout. Edit k, choose filters, then step through epochs.

Epoch update rule (illustrative, not from a source): a prompt that was trained on with 0 < k < 8 gains one correct rollout per epoch; prompts at 0/8 or 8/8 stay where they are.
