Why more inference at a fixed batch size raises staleness

Rocket (p. 7): with more inference servers it takes less time to collect a batch, so in an inference-bound job the time between learner steps Δt shrinks, while a rollout's wall-clock time D is unchanged. The waiting term of staleness is about D / Δt learner steps. Rollout durations are the medians of Rocket Fig. 2. Rocket does not report its step time; the defaults below are Olmo 3's representative step (1,000 s, of which 125 s is training; Olmo 3 report §4.4.3), used only as an example of an inference-bound step. Change them to your own values.
